# INFORMATION-LOSS AUDIT

The packaging objective was preservation, not simplification.

- Raw historical ZIPs are retained byte-for-byte in the History archive.
- Two previously generated research-history/timeline files are retained as stitched text snapshots.
- U2–U7 and Long01/Node3Fa snapshots available on the Project/Library surface are retained in both navigation/tooling form and history snapshots as appropriate.
- Failed routes and corrections are indexed, not deleted.
- The Obsidian vault is referenced, not copied, to avoid duplicating a 213 MB independent knowledge asset.
- The “Dangerous Message” source is explicitly recorded as unavailable for byte-level inclusion in this runtime; no substitute content was fabricated.
- No mathematical conclusion was recomputed during packaging.
