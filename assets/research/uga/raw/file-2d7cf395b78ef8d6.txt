## UGA-C061 — Ordinary Sections Give Terminating Descent and a Quantitative Bound

\operatorname{gldim}A\le(v-1)M.

The all-vertex section hypothesis for the numerical bound, the all-source condition and all n>=M must be retained. The infinite-pd-only theorem does not assert that bound. Path-length grading alone does not imply erasure stability. The Run gives a six-dimensional calibration where the ordinary section fails but a derived section exists.
