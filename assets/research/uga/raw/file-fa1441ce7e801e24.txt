## 1. Ordinary conventions and the scope of the test

A is finite-dimensional elementary over any field k, J=rad A,
L_y=Ae_y/Je_y and R_z=e_zA/e_zJ. Set

\[
\beta_{zy}(n)=\dim_k\operatorname{Tor}^A_n(R_z,L_y),\qquad
D(y,z)=\dim_k e_yAe_z.
\tag{1.1}
\]

Infinite + means one fixed pair with D(y,z)>0 and beta_zy(n)>0
in infinitely many ordinary degrees. Define separately

\[
I_L=\{y:\operatorname{pd}_A L_y=\infty\},\qquad
I_R=\{z:\operatorname{pd}_{A^{op}}R_z=\infty\},
\]
\[
H_{zy}=1\iff\beta_{zy}(n)>0\text{ infinitely often},\qquad
W_z=\{y:H_{zy}=1\},\quad T_y=\{z:H_{zy}=1\}.
\tag{1.2}
\]

Actual minimal resolutions imply W_z is nonempty exactly when z in I_R,
and T_y is nonempty exactly when y in I_L. Moreover W_z is contained
in I_L and T_y in I_R. A finite pd on EITHER argument bounds its Tor.
Neither the infinite-pd sets nor their labels are identified.

Reuse only the valid finite-length input from T15: if every composition
factor of a finite module has pd at most d on the stated side, the
module has pd at most d. A projective module can have infinite-pd
factors, so the converse remains prohibited.

The existing Peirce-Elimination-Theorem proves that, under absence of
infinite +, each nonempty persistent row and column has at least two
labels. Section 2 supplies the precise direct ordinary specialization
needed here; this is a reused bridge, not a new singleton-tail theorem.

## 2. Direct ordinary singleton-detector bridge

### Lemma 2.1

If some infinite-pd right simple R_z has W_z={y}, then A is infinite +.

For sufficiently large s, its minimal right projectives Q_n, n>=s,
are all sums of e_yA: all other simple-pair Betti numbers have their
last nonzero degree. Put X=Omega_right^s R_z. This is an actual
nonzero module with a minimal right tail of that type. Ordinary
dimension shifting gives Tor_n^A(X,L_y) nonzero infinitely often.

X is a quotient of Q_s, so each right composition factor R_q of X
has e_yAe_q!=0. Devissage in the RIGHT argument yields, in every n,

\[
\dim\operatorname{Tor}_n^A(X,L_y)
\le\sum_q[X:R_q]\,\beta_{qy}(n).
\tag{2.1}
\]

Finite factors give a fixed q with H_qy=1 and e_yAe_q!=0. This is
the required ordinary supported pair, possibly with q different from z.
QED.

There is a direct LEFT version: if T_y={z} for an infinite-pd L_y,
its high projectives are sums of Ae_z. A real left syzygy X is their
quotient, and Tor(R_z,X) persists. Its composition factors L_q satisfy
e_qAe_z!=0. Ordinary devissage selects a supported pair (R_z,L_q).
This is not an invocation of opposite-infinite-+ invariance.

## 3. A heterogeneous composition aggregate, and full two-source closure

Let x in I_L. Every Omega^n L_x has infinite pd, hence has at least
one factor with label in I_L. There is a finite common pd bound for
the other left simples; omitting all labels in I_L would contradict
infinite pd. Therefore the actual cover sequence gives, in EVERY n>=0,

\[
\boxed{\sum_z\left(\sum_{y\in I_L}D(y,z)\right)\beta_{zx}(n)
=\sum_{y\in I_L}\bigl(\dim e_y\Omega^n L_x+
                           \dim e_y\Omega^{n+1}L_x\bigr)\ge2.}
\tag{3.1}
\]

This aggregate alone is NOT a supported Tor witness: it may pair the
Tor source x with the physical support of another infinite-pd y.
The next argument proves the missing alignment when |I_L|<=2.

### Theorem 3.1 — Arbitrary-size closure with at most two infinite-pd left simples

If |I_L|<=2, then A satisfies Universal Gap A, for every finite
vertex number and every field.

#### Proof

If I_L is empty, all simples have a common finite pd bound, hence
gldim A is finite. Otherwise suppose gldim A is infinite.

If any z in I_R has a singleton W_z, Lemma 2.1 already proves A
infinite +. In the remaining case every nonempty W_z has at least
two labels. Each is contained in I_L. Thus |I_L|=2 and

\[
W_z=I_L\qquad(z\in I_R).
\tag{3.2}
\]

Fix x in I_L. Equation (3.1) and finite labels give a fixed z such
that sum_{y in I_L}D(y,z)>0 and beta_zx(n)>0 infinitely often.
Such z is in I_R. Choose y in I_L with D(y,z)>0. By (3.2), H_zy=1.
Consequently the SAME pair (R_z,L_y) has physical reverse support
and persistent ordinary Tor. QED.

For an alternative contradiction audit, no infinite + and (3.2)
would imply D(y,z)=0 for all y in I_L, z in I_R. Above the finite
maximum of the right pds outside I_R, every P_n(L_x) uses only
Ae_z with z in I_R. Its quotient Omega^n L_x then has no infinite-pd
left factor, so has finite pd, a contradiction. This uses the
finite-factor pd implication only in its valid direction.

Theorem 3.1 closes a whole multiple-infinite-simple branch; it is
not merely a necessary condition. It guarantees a fixed persistent
pair, not a diagonal pair or nonzero values at every degree. T15's
stronger every-degree matched aggregate for one source is unchanged.

### Theorem 3.2 — Direct right-side closure

If |I_R|<=2, the same Gap-A conclusion holds by direct ordinary
right resolutions.

For x in I_R, every real Omega_right^n R_x contains a factor in I_R,
and the exact right cover sequence gives

\[
\sum_y\left(\sum_{z\in I_R}D(y,z)\right)\beta_{xy}(n)
=\sum_{z\in I_R}\bigl(\dim(\Omega_{right}^n R_x)e_z+
                       \dim(\Omega_{right}^{n+1}R_x)e_z\bigr)\ge2.
\tag{3.3}
\]

If a persistent column T_y is a singleton, use the direct LEFT
version of Lemma 2.1. Otherwise every nonempty T_y, contained in I_R,
equals I_R. Finite labels in (3.3) select y with persistent beta_xy
and D(y,z)>0 for some z in I_R. Since T_y=I_R, beta_zy also persists.
This gives support D(y,z)>0 for exactly Tor(R_z,L_y). QED.

No opposite-+ theorem or equality of I_L,I_R occurs. No universal
finite-window, vertex-only bound, or extra quantitative bound is claimed.

## 4. Known ordinary radical moves do not decrease this count

### Theorem 4.1 — Individual finite/infinite pd labels are preserved

Let K be an actual radical square-zero ideal, C=A/K, with K left
C-projective and actual projective-column matrix T acyclic. Write
q for its longest path, as in C076. For EVERY labelled left simple,

\[
\operatorname{pd}_A L_y<\infty\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{pd}_C L_y<\infty.
\tag{4.1}
\]

In the finite cases a=pd_A L_y and c=pd_C L_y satisfy

\[
c\le a+2q,\qquad a\le c+q.
\tag{4.2}
\]

#### Proof

If a is finite, all ordinary A-Tor(R_z,L_y) vanish for n>=a+1
and EVERY z. In particular C076's entire F-reachable carrier
hypothesis holds for each z. Its comparison yields C-Tor zero
for n>=a+1+2q, proving c<=a+2q by the minimal C resolution.

Conversely C072/C076 give pd_A C<=q. Inflate a finite C-projective
resolution of L_y of length c. Each term is in add(C), so its
ambient A-pd is at most q. The ordinary resolution bound gives
a<=c+q. K contained in J preserves all simple labels. QED.

With right C-projective K, the direct right C076 comparison proves
the analogous equivalence for each RIGHT simple and the same bounds.
It does not prove preservation of the opposite chosen side.

Therefore C074's left-projective square-zero/tensor-square-zero
badness-preserving steps and every left-projective step of C075 keep
the left infinite-simple count EXACTLY constant. They genuinely
terminate by dim A or the remaining actual kernel-type count, not
by decreasing |I_L|. C076's more general comparison has the same
count invariance but still lacks general physical support transfer.

The other main radical bridges have the same chosen-side limitation.
C072's eventual SAME-PAIR ordinary Tor isomorphism preserves both
finite/infinite simple-pd partitions. For C069, its ordinary bar
retract gives pd_C L_y<=pd_A L_y; if the actual K has finite LEFT
ambient A-pd, then pd_A C is finite and inflation of a finite
C-resolution gives finite A-pd. Thus left labels again agree.
With finite RIGHT ambient kernel pd the corresponding right labels
agree, using direct right resolutions. These conclusions use exactly

The unchanged connected A12 -> C7 from C075, Section 4.2, is already

## 5. Deleting an actually infinite-pd vertex can create more infinite simples

This new actual calibration breaks automatic count monotonicity even

### 5.1 A finite-gldim fourteen-dimensional base

Let R=k[t]/(t^3), M_i=k[t]/(t^i), i=1,2,3, M=M1 direct sum M2
direct sum M3, and

\[
B=\operatorname{End}_R(M)^{op}.
\tag{5.1}
\]

Its elementary Cartan dimensions are dim Hom_R(M_i,M_j)=min(i,j),
so dim B=14. A basis h_ij^s of Hom_R(M_i,M_j) sends 1 to t^s,
where max(0,j-i)<=s<j. In B (opposite composition),

\[
h_{ij}^s h_{jl}^u=h_{il}^{s+u}\ (s+u<l),
\qquad\text{and all other products are zero}.
\tag{5.2}
\]

The vertex idempotent is h_ii^0. This supplies a literal all-field
multiplication, including primitive vertices, not just a quiver graph.

Left P_i=Hom_R(M,M_i) are B-projectives. The following real short
exact R-module sequences give their simple resolutions:

\[
0\to M_1\xrightarrow{t}M_2\xrightarrow{mod\ t}M_1\to0,
\]
\[
0\to M_2\xrightarrow{g}M_1\oplus M_3\xrightarrow{h}M_2\to0,
\quad g(u)=(u\bmod t,tu),\quad h(v,w)=tv-w\bmod t^2.
\tag{5.3}
\]

Apply Hom_R(M,-). It is left exact. In the first sequence, every
noninvertible map from an indecomposable M_i into M1 factors through
M2 -> M1. In the second, maps from M1 and M3 into M2 factor through
h, and the radical endomorphism t of M2 factors through M3. Thus
the images at P1 and P2 are EXACTLY their B radicals. The remaining
cokernel is the stated left simple, and all maps are minimal:

\[
0\to P_1\to P_2\to P_1\to L_1^B\to0,
\]
\[
0\to P_2\to P_1\oplus P_3\to P_2\to L_2^B\to0.
\tag{5.4}
\]

Also every noninvertible map into M3 has image in tM3=M2, so

\[
0\to P_2\xrightarrow{t}P_3\to L_3^B\to0.
\tag{5.5}
\]

Consequently the three left pds are 2,2,1; in particular gd B=2.
These are literal module proofs, with no appeal to a recalled
Auslander-algebra theorem. For the right covers, the actual basis map
h_ij^s -> h_ji^{s+i-j} reverses multiplication in (5.2) and fixes the
idempotents. It transports (5.4)--(5.5) to actual right projective
complexes, giving right pds 2,2,1; these are also checked below.
This is an explicit ordinary chain comparison for this B, not any
universal opposite-infinite-+ assertion.

For D=B/Be3B, morphisms between M1,M2 factoring through M3 form
precisely the one-dimensional span of t in End_R(M2); the other
three retained Hom spaces lose no dimension. Indeed an injection
into M3 has image in tM3, or t^2 M3 from M1, and the respective
quotients give the stated products. Thus D has dimension four,
with two opposite arrows, both products zero. Its actual simple
syzygies alternate L1 and L2, so both left and right pds are infinite.

### 5.2 A connected twenty-two-dimensional algebra

Let Gamma=k[t_v]/(t_v^2) and G=Gamma/(t_v). Set

\[
V=G\otimes_k e_3B,\qquad
A=\begin{pmatrix}B&0\\V&\Gamma\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{5.6}
\]

V is an actual (Gamma,B)-bimodule, with t_v V=0 and the regular
right e3B action. Its dimension is 1+2+3=6, so dim A=22. The
ordinary triangular multiplication is associative over every field.
The new vertex v is joined to B by the nonzero top of V at 3;
the base has both consecutive connections. Thus A is connected.

Induction A(e1+e2+e3) tensor_B - is exact: as a right B module
this inducing bimodule is B direct sum V, and V is projective.
It sends P_j to Ae_j, and sends L_j^B to the actual A simple L_j
for j=1,2, because e3 L_j^B=0. Thus pd_A L1=pd_A L2=2 by the
induced minimal resolutions (5.4).

For L3, the minimal cover has the actual splitting

\[
\boxed{\Omega_A L_3\cong Ae_2\oplus L_v,\qquad
       \Omega_A L_v\cong L_v.}
\tag{5.7}
\]

To check the first splitting physically, embed Ae2 into rad(Ae3)
by right multiplication with h_23^1. On the B part this is (5.5).
On V e2 it maps the basis exponents 0,1 to exponents 1,2 in V e3.
The remaining exponent-zero vector of V e3 is a separate copy of G
at vertex v; all other left vertex actions vanish on it and t_v
kills it. Thus it is exactly L_v and the direct sum is A-linear.
The second equality uses Ae_v=(0,Gamma) and its actual t_v radical.

This proves, in ALL ordinary degrees and over every field,

\[
I_L(A)=\{3,v\},\qquad
\beta_{v,3}(n)=1\ (n\ge1),\qquad
\beta_{v,v}(n)=1\ (n\ge0).
\tag{5.8}
\]

For the direct right audit, the old projectives e_jA equal e_jB,
so R1,R2,R3 have finite right pd. The actual radical of e_vA is
the direct sum V direct sum kt_v, with right modules

\[
\Omega_{right}R_v\cong e_3A\oplus R_v.
\tag{5.9}
\]

t_v V=0 proves this splitting. Every further right syzygy is the
same e3A direct sum R_v: although e3A is projective, a new copy
reappears on taking the syzygy of R_v. Therefore I_R(A)={v}.
The direct right resolution also gives beta_v3(n)=beta_vv(n)=1
for every n>=1, agreeing with the left calculation. No right
projective term is incorrectly discarded from that recurrence.

### 5.3 The actual deletion and the increased counts

\tag{5.10}

\[
|I_L(A)|=2<3=|I_L(C)|,\qquad
|I_R(A)|=1<3=|I_R(C)|,
\tag{5.11}

## 8. Verdict on the proposed global structure and next research

The global alternative is still OPEN. If it held for every hypothetical
bad A on one fixed chosen side, induction on the finite count, with T15
or Theorem 3.1 as base, would prove Universal Gap A. A genuine refutation
therefore cannot be supplied by an already-infinite-+ calibration.

What has been proved is the full all-size branch |I_L|<=2 OR |I_R|<=2,
not a refinement of a hypothetical higher-output tail. What has been
broken is the assumption that existing radical moves decrease that
count, and that deletion of an infinite-pd source cannot create new
infinite-pd labels. The precise obstruction is ordinary homology:
quotient-side minimal resolutions can become nonterminating even for
retained simples with finite ambient pd; no Tor retract preserves their
finite pd in (5.10). Left-projective acyclic radical moves, in contrast,
preserve the whole left finite/infinite partition exactly.

Thus an eligible vertex count reduction must prove BOTH supported
ordinary return transfer and no new infinite-pd labels (or a sufficient
net count estimate). C061 supplies these under actual ordinary sections.
General sections or a replacement comparison are not established.
Known C072/C074/C075 reductions can still close cases by dimension/type
descent; their success should not be relabelled as infinite-simple count
descent. C076's remaining physical-support bridge is unchanged.

Continue from the genuine multiple-source obstruction: in (3.1), the
persistent detector for source x may physically support another infinite
source y that it does not persistently detect. This is a failure of
ordinary same-pair alignment, not proof that the universal alternative
is false. Any next bridge must kill that misalignment or construct an
actual badness-preserving reduction with proved eligibility. General
Target A, local-trivial, supported-weight and higher-output Target-B
branches remain open and retained.
