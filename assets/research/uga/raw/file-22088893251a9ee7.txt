- “The comparison problem is still abstract.” **Status: Method shortcut, not a proof step.** `9b661439db7f6ae9` now supplies the actual canonical maps; future arguments should use their kernels and not arbitrary sequences.

**Strategies not to pursue**

**Closure criterion.** A proof must explicitly show why the two verified counterexamples cannot arise from the minimal dead-zone canonical comparison. This bridge depends on Bridge 1 but its invariant design is parallelizable.
