# 15. Minimal-resolution normal form for an actual quotient

There is a second actual-realization constraint which does not appear in an arbitrary two-stage object.

Take a minimal right \(D\)-projective resolution

\[
\cdots\to P_2\to P_1\to P_0\to S\to0.
\]

Tensor with the left \(D\)-module \(S\).

Only projective summands at the vertex \(e\) survive.

Thus

\[
C_\bullet=P_\bullet\otimes_DS
\]

is a minimal complex of finite free right \(S\)-modules.

Its homology is

\[
H_0(C)=S,\qquad
H_1(C)=0,\qquad
H_2(C)=M,
\]

and all higher homology vanishes.

Since \(S\) and \(M\) are projective right \(S\)-modules, minimality forces the complex itself into the normal form

\[
\boxed{
C_0=S,\qquad
C_1=0,\qquad
C_2=S^\rho,\qquad
C_n=0\quad(n\ge3).
}
\tag{15.1}
\]

Indeed:

- \(H_0=S\) projective forces \(\operatorname{im}d_1=0\);
- \(H_1=0\) then forces \(C_1=0\);
- \(H_2=S^\rho\) projective forces \(\operatorname{im}d_3=0\);
- \(H_3=0\) then forces \(C_3=0\);
- induction kills all higher terms.

Consequently, in the minimal right \(D\)-resolution of \(S\), the \(e\)-projective occurs:

- once in degree \(0\);
- zero times in degree \(1\);
- exactly \(\rho\) times in degree \(2\);
- zero times in every degree \(\ge3\).

The same statement holds for the minimal left resolution.

Thus every actual amplitude-two screener must have a:

\[
\boxed{
\textbf{bidirectional isolated second return to }e.
}
\]

This is a concrete actual-quotient condition not present in an abstract two-row spectral sequence.

# 20. Why the actual near-miss does not screen

The crucial defect is the opposite \(S\)-action on

\[
M=\operatorname{Tor}^D_2(S,S).
\]

The left action of \(x\in S\) is computed by lifting left multiplication by \(x\) to the resolution (19.1).

At degree \(0\), use left multiplication by \(x\) on \(eD\).

Since

\[
xa=0,
\]

the degree-\(1\) lift may be chosen zero.

Since

\[
eD\xrightarrow{b}fD
\]

is injective, the degree-\(2\) lift is then also zero.

Hence \(x\) acts trivially on \(\operatorname{Tor}_2\) from the left.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
{}_SM
\cong
k\oplus k,
}
\]

while

\[
M_S\cong S.
\]

So \(M\) is projective on the right but nonprojective on the left.

This violates the separate-perfection condition forced by hypothetical no-return.

The ambient diagonal simple also visibly recurs: the radical of \(eD\) contains the right submodule

\[
xD\cong k_e.
\]

Thus:

\[
\operatorname{Tor}^D_n(k_e,k_e)\neq0
\]

in arbitrarily high degree.

This seven-dimensional algebra is an exact actual calibration:

\[
\boxed{
\text{amplitude }2\text{ is realizable, but one-sided freeness is not enough for screening}.
}
\]
