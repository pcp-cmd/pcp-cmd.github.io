## 1. Exact meanings and reused ordinary assets

A is elementary finite-dimensional over any field k. L_y is LEFT simple,
R_z RIGHT simple, I_L and I_R are their separately defined infinite-pd
sets. Physical reverse support for Tor^A(R_z,L_y) is e_yAe_z.

For a LEFT projective Ae_z define its infinite-factor carrier

\[
C_z^L=\{j\in I_L:e_jAe_z\ne0\}.
\tag{1.1}
\]

For a RIGHT projective e_yA use

\[
C_y^R=\{j\in I_R:e_yAe_j\ne0\}.
\tag{1.2}
\]

For a module, its carrier means the labels of its infinite-pd simple
COMPOSITION FACTORS. For an actual recurrent projective tail, its
carrier means the union of these carriers for the projective types
occurring infinitely often. These are different objects. A strict drop
for a map IMAGE does not establish a drop for its minimal projective
cover, its syzygies, or a recurrent tail.

Use C080, C088, C089, the actual Peirce-block proof and ordinary
finite-length Tor inequalities. The high tail is first cut beyond the

The requested bi-singular purification is not an established ordinary

## 2. A positive arbitrary-size terminal bridge

### Theorem 2.1 — A carrier-dominating recurrent detector forces ordinary visible return (C090)

Let K be an actual persistent Peirce component in a sufficiently high
minimal LEFT simple resolution, and let

\[
S_K=\bigcup_{z\in K}C_z^L.
\tag{2.1}
\]

If some recurrent projective type q in K satisfies

\[
C_q^L=S_K,
\tag{2.2}
\]

then there is a fixed j with e_jAe_q nonzero and
Tor^A_n(R_q,L_j) nonzero for infinitely many n. No bound on either
infinite-source count or on total vertex number is required.

#### Proof

The literal high block is exact and minimal. Augment it at degree s
by X=coker(P^K_{s+1}->P^K_s). This is a finite LEFT module whose actual
minimal resolution is that block with the degree shift. Since q really
recurs, Tor^A_n(R_q,X) is nonzero infinitely often. X is a quotient of
P^K_s, so its infinite left simple factors lie in S_K.

Let d be the maximum pd of the finitely many finite-pd LEFT simples,
taking d=0 when the set is empty. Ordinary long exact Tor sequences
along an actual composition series give, for n>d,

\[
\dim\operatorname{Tor}^A_n(R_q,X)
\le\sum_{j\in S_K}[X:L_j]
       \dim\operatorname{Tor}^A_n(R_q,L_j).
\tag{2.3}
\]

If S_K were empty X would have finite pd, contradicting recurrence.
Otherwise finite pigeonhole in (2.3) selects a fixed j in S_K with
persistent ordinary Tor. By (2.2) e_jAe_q is nonzero. This is the SAME
detector and supported simple pair. QED.

The RIGHT proof is direct: use an actual high RIGHT block, a recurrent
projective e_qA with C_q^R equal to the union, and its genuine right
block module X. Tor(X,L_q) persists, finite RIGHT factors die uniformly,
and a fixed R_j has Tor(R_j,L_q) persistent with e_qAe_j nonzero.

Constant per-projective infinite carriers are a special case. The
condition only needs ONE dominating recurrent projective. A constant
UNION of carriers alone is insufficient; every finite tail has such a
union. C087's singleton-carrier block lemma is included in this proof.

Together with C088 this gives an arbitrary-size terminating class:
if checked closed finite-side block deletions lead to an actual block
with a dominating recurrent detector, its visible ordinary pair lifts
through every all-degree comparison. Each nonempty deletion lowers
vertex number and dimension; positive opposite-side intersection
also lowers the indicated infinite count. This asserts neither
automatic deletion availability nor universal terminal dominance.

## 3. Smaller image carrier does not give smaller recurrent carrier

### Family 3.1 — A radical-square-zero oriented p-cycle, every p>=3

Let \(\mathsf C_p\) have identities e_i and
alpha_i in e_i A e_{i+1}, indices modulo p, and all radical products
zero. Its dimension is 2p. Actual syzygies are

\[
\Omega R_i=R_{i+1},\qquad \Omega L_i=L_{i-1}.
\tag{3.1}
\]

Thus I_L=I_R is ALL p labels. The actual minimal RIGHT R_i resolution
has Q_n=e_{i+n}A; its differential is left multiplication by
alpha_{i+n-1}. The adjacent projective carriers are

\[
C_{i+n}^R=\{i+n,i+n+1\},\qquad
C_{i+n-1}^R=\{i+n-1,i+n\}.
\tag{3.2}
\]

They are incomparable. The differential image is the infinite-pd
simple R_{i+n}, with singleton carrier equal to their intersection.
This is a genuine strict IMAGE-carrier decrease. Its actual projective
cover immediately has the two-label carrier again, and (3.1) cycles
through all p labels. There is no strict recurrent-tail decrease.

In fact this obstruction holds for ANY finite module of infinite pd
over this algebra. Its first minimal syzygy is a nonzero semisimple
submodule of Q_0 J, since J^2=0. Every nonzero simple summand has the
actual cyclic syzygies (3.1). Thus EVERY projective type occurs
infinitely often in its minimal tail. The recurrent carrier is ALL p
labels, so no proper-carrier infinite minimal tail exists at all.

Finite-simple purification does nothing because all labels are
infinite on both sides. Ordinary Tor(R_i,L_i)=k in every positive
degree divisible by p, with physical diagonal support. Hence this is
a counterexample to the unqualified strict carrier lemma, not to Gap A.

## 4. Uniformly projective cross images cannot simply be eliminated

### Family 4.1 — Actual projective-image obstruction, every p>=3

Let \(\mathsf D_p\) have vertices 0,...,p-2,v=p-1, identities, and

\[
t\in e_vAe_v,\quad a_i\in e_vAe_i,\quad
b_i\in e_iAe_v,\quad c_i\in e_vAe_v.
\tag{4.1}
\]

The ONLY nonzero radical products are a_i b_i=c_i; all others are
zero. This is associative with J^3=0 and dimension 4p-2. For RIGHT
projectives P_i=e_iA and P_v=e_vA,

\[
P_i=k e_i\oplus k b_i,\quad
a_iA=k a_i\oplus k c_i\cong P_i,
\]
\[
\Omega R_i=R_v,\qquad
\Omega R_v=\bigoplus_{i<p-1}P_i\oplus R_v.
\tag{4.2}
\]

On the LEFT, b_i,c_i span a copy of Ae_i inside Ae_v, giving
Omega L_i=L_v and Omega L_v=(sum Ae_i) direct sum L_v. These actual
kernel identities prove all simples infinite-pd on BOTH sides.

The actual minimal RIGHT resolution of R_v has Q_0=P_v and

\[
Q_n=P_v\oplus\bigoplus_i P_i\quad(n\ge1).
\tag{4.3}
\]

Each d_n injects P_i isomorphically onto a_iA in the preceding P_v,
and sends the P_v summand by t into k t in that same summand. All
maps have radical entries, hence are minimal. Their kernel is
rad(P_v) in the source P_v; the next image is exactly that kernel.
This proves exactness in ALL degrees.

The projective carriers are C_i^R={i,v} and C_v^R=ALL p labels.
Cross maps P_i->P_v occur in every positive degree and have image
of RIGHT pd zero. Their pds have a uniform bound of zero.

Delete those correction summands/maps and keep only the actual
projectives P_v with differential t. The resulting complex has

\[
H_n(P_v\xrightarrow{t}P_v\xrightarrow{t}\cdots)
\cong\bigoplus_i P_i\ne0\quad(n\ge1).
\tag{4.4}
\]

Indeed ker(t)=rad(P_v)=sum a_iA direct sum kt and im(t)=kt.
Thus even projective homology survives in arbitrarily high degrees.
Finite pd of each local error does not make this unbounded collection
of errors a bounded perfect correction.

The exact resolution (4.3) gives Tor_n(R_v,L_i)=k for every n>=1,
and e_iAe_v=k b_i is nonzero. These are SUPPORTED ordinary tails.
The pruned terms P_v tensor L_i are zero in every degree. Pruning
therefore loses an actual supported ordinary tail, in addition to
destroying exactness. It cannot compute ordinary Tor over A.

No smaller-carrier infinite minimal tail rescues the alternative.
Any proper-carrier tail must omit P_v. Between the other projectives
all radical Hom spaces vanish: off-diagonal old-old Peirce entries
are zero and each old diagonal is just k. Every remaining minimal
differential is therefore zero, incompatible with a nonzero exact
infinite tail. This holds for any finite module's minimal tail.

The images a_iA are projective, but are NOT split projective summands
of the indecomposable P_v containing them in its radical. No invertible
matrix entry is available for ordinary cancellation. A derived repair
using nonprojective quotient terms must retain their ordinary derived
tensor corrections; it is not an unchanged A-projective resolution.

This algebra already has supported ordinary return. Moreover its
recurrent P_v dominates the whole carrier, so Theorem 2.1 handles it
directly. Deleting its essential projective images is unnecessary.

## 5. Finite-FACTOR images need not supply any eligible closed block

### Family 5.1 — Every p>=2, exactly p LEFT infinite labels and p+1 RIGHT infinite labels (C092)

Start with the nine-dimensional cyclic Nakayama algebra B on f,v:

\[
a\in e_fBe_v,\qquad b\in e_vBe_f,\qquad baba=0.
\tag{5.1}
\]

The literal basis is e_f,e_v,a,b,ab,ba,aba,bab,abab. Products are
path concatenation whenever the result is in this basis. In particular
abab is NONZERO and baba is zero. Its LEFT projectives have uniserial
factor strings

\[
P_f: f,v,f,v,f,\qquad P_v:v,f,v,f.
\tag{5.2}
\]

Right multiplication by b identifies P_v with rad(P_f). Hence

\[
\Omega L_f=P_v,\quad \operatorname{pd}L_f=1.
\tag{5.3}
\]

Put U_1=rad(P_v)=span(a,ba,aba) and U_2=span(ba,aba). Actual minimal
covers give

\[
\Omega L_v=U_1,\quad \Omega U_1=U_2,\quad \Omega U_2=U_2.
\tag{5.4}
\]

For Omega U_1, the kernel in P_f is span(bab,abab), isomorphic to U_2
by right multiplication by b. For Omega U_2, the cover P_v maps its
generator to ba and its kernel is span(ba,aba) again. Thus L_v really
has infinite ordinary pd. No prefix test is used.

Adjoin q=p-1 vertices w_r, each with a dual-number corner
Gamma_r=k[t_r]/t_r^2, and m_r in e_fAe_{w_r}. All radical products
involving m_r or t_r are zero. This is the literal triangular algebra

\[
\mathsf E_p=
\begin{pmatrix}B&M\\0&\prod_{r=1}^{p-1}\Gamma_r\end{pmatrix},
\qquad M=\bigoplus_r L_f\otimes_k G_r,
\tag{5.5}
\]

where G_r is the RIGHT Gamma_r simple. It is connected, has p+1
vertices, dimension 9+3(p-1)=3p+6, and J^5=0. Old LEFT projectives
and their resolutions are literally unchanged. For a chosen w,

\[
P_w=Ae_w=k e_w\oplus k t_w\oplus k m_w,
\qquad \Omega L_w=L_f\oplus L_w.
\tag{5.6}
\]

Consequently

\[
I_L=\{v,w_1,\ldots,w_{p-1}\},\quad F_L=\{f\},\quad
\operatorname{pd}_A L_f=1.
\tag{5.7}
\]

These statements are exact ordinary ones: (5.3) terminates, (5.4)
returns, and (5.6) contains a direct copy of L_w at every iteration.

On the RIGHT, P_v^R=e_vA still equals e_vB. Left multiplication by a
identifies it with aB in rad(e_fA). The other radical summands are
m_r A=R_{w_r}. Therefore

\[
\Omega R_f=P_v^R\oplus\bigoplus_r R_{w_r},\qquad
\Omega R_{w_r}=R_{w_r}.
\tag{5.8}
\]

For R_v let V_1=span(b,ba,bab) inside e_vA and V_2=span(ba,bab).
Their genuine minimal covers give

\[
\Omega R_v=V_1,\quad
\Omega V_1=V_2\oplus\bigoplus_r R_{w_r},\quad
\Omega V_2=V_2.
\tag{5.9}
\]

The copy of V_2 in the first kernel is span(aba,abab) in e_fA,
via left multiplication by a; the extra m_r are independent simples.
Equations (5.8)–(5.9) prove directly that

\[
I_R=\{f,v,w_1,\ldots,w_{p-1}\},\qquad F_R=\varnothing.
\tag{5.10}
\]

#### The cross maps occur in the actual recurrent minimal tail

The LEFT L_w resolution is

\[
Q_0=P_w,\quad Q_1=P_f\oplus P_w,\quad
Q_n=P_v\oplus P_f\oplus P_w\quad(n\ge2).
\tag{5.11}
\]

For all n>=2 its three nonzero differential blocks are

\[
P_v\xrightarrow{\,b\,}P_f,\qquad
P_f\xrightarrow{\,m_w\,}P_w,\qquad
P_w\xrightarrow{\,t_w\,}P_w,
\tag{5.12}
\]

where LEFT maps act by RIGHT multiplication. The middle map has
image k m_w=L_f. Its kernel is rad(P_f)=P_v; the last map has kernel
L_f direct sum L_w, and the first map is injective. Thus each whole
kernel equals the next whole image. All entries b,m_w,t_w lie in J,
so (5.11) is exact and minimal in EVERY degree.

Its infinite-factor carriers are

\[
C_f^L=C_v^L=\{v\},\qquad C_{w_r}^L=\{w_r\}.
\tag{5.13}
\]

Hence P_f->P_w is a genuine cross map between distinct infinite
carriers. Its image contains ONLY a finite-pd simple factor and has
pd one, uniformly, at every positive resolution degree.

#### No eligible finite-side closed block exists on either side

Any LEFT-finite block containing that image's vertex must be F={f},
because (5.7) lists ALL finite LEFT simples. But

\[
e_vAe_f\supset k b\ne0,
\tag{5.14}
\]

so gAf is nonzero: P_f's cover contains the infinite LEFT label v.
No LEFT-finite projective-cover-closed block can contain f. There
is no nonempty RIGHT-finite block at all by (5.10). Therefore no
C088 deletion of either side is available, despite the recurrent
finite-FACTOR cross images.

Deleting their correction branch P_f and P_v from (5.11) leaves
P_w with differential t_w, whose positive-degree homology is L_f
in EVERY degree: ker(t_w)=L_f+L_w and im(t_w)=L_w. It is not exact.
If all terms are kept but the middle maps alone are zeroed, the
two independent branches have homology L_f direct sum L_f in every
high degree. Neither operation is an ordinary resolution.

From (5.11), Tor_n(R_f,L_w)=k for n>=1 and Tor_n(R_v,L_w)=k for
n>=2; pruning loses both. These two pairs are physically invisible,
since e_wAe_f=e_wAe_v=0. Separately Tor_n(R_w,L_w)=k in EVERY degree
has diagonal physical support. Thus the family is already infinite +.
It refutes automatic finite-image-to-closed-block existence and literal
ordinary elimination; it does not refute a badness-specific alternative.

The factor/cover distinction is essential: L_f is finite-pd, although
its projective cover P_f contains the infinite-pd factor L_v. Bounded
pd does not pass to composition factors. Every r contributes the same
actual recurrent phenomenon, so the obstruction has no count cutoff.

## 6. Exact availability criterion for the valid block mechanism

For a finite LEFT factor label u, form the least set H_L(u) that
contains u and is closed under adding every label j with e_jAe_z
nonzero for a label z already in the set. This is the literal
projective-cover closure, with no homological inference from its graph.

There exists a C088 finite-LEFT closed block containing a support set
U if and only if the union H_L(U) avoids I_L. Necessity follows because
any closed block containing U contains its entire closure. Sufficiency:
that closure is itself finite-LEFT and cover-closed; if A has an
infinite LEFT simple it is proper. A finite-factor image alone specifies
U contained in F_L, not H_L(U) contained in F_L. Family E_p fails in one
step f->v by (5.14).

The union of ALL eligible finite-LEFT blocks is itself the maximal
eligible finite-LEFT block. Removing it by C088 already performs every
available same-side block deletion at once. No new eligible LEFT block
appears solely from that deletion: any original closure path to an
infinite LEFT label cannot pass through the removed closed block and
then leave it. A RIGHT block deletion can change this conclusion by
removing an infinite LEFT label; each such move still has the exact
proved C088 count and comparison control. The RIGHT criterion is proved
directly using entries e_zAe_j for RIGHT covers.

This gives a concrete eligibility test and maximal valid deletion, not
an assertion of universal availability. It replaces unsuccessful
individual deletion choices without introducing a new derived invariant.
