- Treat every sentence above as source metadata/claim until reproduced and

## Required provenance discipline

All Cycle-2 findings are **literature reproduction**, **literature-inspired**, or
**source audit**. Do not label them independent. Cite exact arXiv version and
page/theorem identifiers in `external_refs` when submitting facts.

## Branches

1. **Source and dependency audit.** Establish exact version, theorem statement,
   construction chain, load-bearing cited results, and any corrections or later
   discussion. Separate source assertions from checked statements.
2. **Ordinary construction and non-smoothness reproduction.** Reconstruct the
   final algebra and the equivalence/nonzero-singularity argument proving finite
   dimensionality, ordinary status, and infinite global dimension.
3. **Hochschild-vanishing reproduction and adversarial audit.** Verify the
   singularity-category Hochschild comparison, phantom/periodization
   acyclicity, degree shifts, and every positive degree; search actively for a
   broken enhancement, quotient, or low-degree interface.

## Verification standard

- Prefer small facts whose hypotheses and conclusions type-check independently.
- A cited theorem may be used only with a precise source and matched hypotheses.
- The final counterexample theorem is not accepted until the ordinary-algebra,
  infinite-global-dimension, and Hochschild-vanishing chains are all closed.
- Any inability to reconstruct an explicit algebra or a load-bearing theorem is
  a reportable gap, not permission to trust the paper.
