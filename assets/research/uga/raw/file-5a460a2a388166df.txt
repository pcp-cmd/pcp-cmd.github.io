## 1. Hypotheses: an order on ACTUAL positive ordinary Ext

Let A be elementary finite-dimensional over k, F={f_1,...,f_m} a nonempty
proper block, e=1-sum_F e_f and B=eAe. Require finite LEFT pds d_i of

\[
d=\max_i d_i,\quad r=\max_i r_i,\quad s=\min(d,r),
\quad w=\max(s-1,0).
\tag{1.1}
\]

Require the order to satisfy

\[
\operatorname{Ext}^p_A(L_{f_i},L_{f_j})=0
\quad(p\ge1,\ i\ge j).
\tag{1.2}
\]

Actual positive Ext may be nonzero from an EARLIER source to a LATER

not be semisimple. This is an order on ordinary Ext GROUPS, not a
quiver/support order or nilpotence of their Yoneda products. Only
degrees 1 through s need checking: left source and right dual-target

No physical cover closure or corner-module perfection is assumed.

## 2. Cohomology carriers terminate in a bounded ordinary window (C099)

#### Refined ordinary head bounds

For each target f_j define a finite recursive bound

\[
b_j=\max\bigl(\{r_j\}\cup
 \{b_i+p-1:i<j,\ p\ge1,
                  \operatorname{Ext}^p_A(L_{f_i},L_{f_j})\ne0\}\bigr).
\tag{2.2}
\]

There is no recursion cycle by (1.2), and p<=s. Each chain makes at
most m-1 strict vertex transitions, each consuming at most w degrees;
therefore max b_j<=a_L. We will prove the stronger assertion

\[
[H_n(C_M):L_{f_j}]=0\quad(n>b_j).
\tag{2.3}
\]

This is a bound used in the termination proof, not a newly proposed
derived invariant. The strict measure is the number of eligible

#### Proof of (2.3) with actual finite ordinary windows

Let H_n=H^{-n}C_M. As in T25, each H_n is finite with F factors;
pd_LEFT H_n<=d. For target L_fj, finite-dimensional ordinary duality
and RIGHT pd R_fj<=r_j imply Ext^p_A(H_n,L_fj)=0 for p>r_j. Thus
the ordinary hyper-Ext page for this target has only 0<=p<=s.

Fix degree n and use the FINITE cohomology truncation [-n,0]: lower
degrees and their shift by one have zero maps to L_fj[n] by ordinary
t-structure orthogonality. The convergent finite spectral sequence has

\[
E_2^{p,q}=\operatorname{Ext}^p_A(H_q,L_{f_j}),
\quad d_\ell:(p,q)\longrightarrow(p+\ell,q-\ell+1).
\tag{2.4}
\]

Its (0,n) Hom edge has NO incoming differential. An outgoing screen
must have 2<=ell<=s and target a subquotient of

\[
\operatorname{Ext}^{\ell}_A(H_{n-\ell+1},L_{f_j}).
\tag{2.5}
\]

Induct in j. If n>b_j, every earlier f_i with a possible degree-ell
Ext into f_j satisfies n-ell+1>b_i. Its composition factor is absent
from H_(n-ell+1) by the induction hypothesis. All remaining factors
have zero degree-ell Ext into L_fj by (1.2) or the definition of b_j.
VALID source composition-series Ext devissage makes (2.5) zero.
Consequently the entire Hom edge survives to the abutment.

Chain adjunction to the deleted target gives

\[
\operatorname{Hom}_{D(A)}(C_M,L_{f_j}[n])
 =\operatorname{Ext}^n_A(M,L_{f_j})=0\quad(n>r_j).
\tag{2.6}
\]

Since n>b_j>=r_j, the surviving edge must be zero:
Hom_A(H_n,L_fj)=0.

A HEAD statement alone would not remove a radical composition factor.
This is exactly why degree-one edges were included in (2.2). Every
f_i with Ext^1(L_fi,L_fj) nonzero has b_j>=b_i, so its factor is also
absent from H_n. Every other factor has zero Ext-one into L_fj. On
the resulting finite composition series, Hom(-,L_fj) is exact and its
dimension is precisely [H_n:L_fj]. Thus the vanished Hom edge implies
the full COMPOSITION component in (2.3) vanishes, even for nonsplit H_n.
This finishes the induction. For n>max b_j every F component is zero,
so H_n=0 and (2.1) follows. QED.
