## 研究目标

### 当前项目主目标：[Universal Gap A · d5eca183c8](#record-d5eca183c89b692751ae618f49a1645b6be10dd63fc0cd2b50e81a183a4a1f65)

For every finite-dimensional basic complex algebra $A$, infinite global dimension implies a fixed pair $i,j$ with $e_jAe_i\ne0$ and $\operatorname{Tor}^A_n(R_i,L_j)\ne0$ for infinitely many $n$.

**适用范围：** All finite-dimensional basic algebras over the complex numbers, with an arbitrary finite number of simple isomorphism classes.

**状态：** 研究目标（不据此判断是否完成）；审核接受；当前所选；当前可用=true；可交付=true

**主目标身份：** complete；首次主目标锚点保持固定，选择其他问题不会替换它。

- **命题：** For every finite-dimensional basic complex algebra $A$, infinite global dimension implies a fixed pair $i,j$ with $e_jAe_i\ne0$ and $\operatorname{Tor}^A_n(R_i,L_j)\ne0$ for infinitely many $n$.
- **定义域：** All finite-dimensional basic algebras over the complex numbers, with an arbitrary finite number of simple isomorphism classes.
- **量词次序：** For every algebra A in the stated domain, if gldim(A) is infinite, there exists one fixed ordered pair (i,j), then infinitely many natural numbers n satisfying the stated support and Tor conditions.
- **假设：** $1=\sum_{i=1}^N e_i$ is a complete primitive orthogonal idempotent decomposition.；$L_j=Ae_j/(\operatorname{rad}A)e_j$ and $R_i=e_iA/e_i(\operatorname{rad}A)$.；No restriction on vertex number, Peirce support, Loewy length or representation type.
- **证据标准：** A complete mathematical proof or a fully specified algebraic counterexample with a rigorous all-degree argument. Finite computations are calibration evidence only; independent review provenance must be explicit.
- **完成标准：** Resolve the universal statement by a proof under exactly these assumptions, or by a rigorous counterexample. Conditional theorems, finite checks and special families are progress, not completion.
