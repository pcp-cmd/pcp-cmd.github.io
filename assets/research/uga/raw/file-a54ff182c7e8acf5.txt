# AGENTS.md — Universal Gap A

This repository is the durable project memory for the Universal Gap A research programme.

## Research environment
- Use the Codex environment's existing built-in MRS.
- Use the already-installed Obsidian Vault Skill when mathematical-method retrieval is useful.
- Do not copy or rebuild the Vault inside this repository.
- The Vault is a general mathematical-method asset, not authority for project-specific facts.

## Before substantive research
1. Read `CURRENT_STATE.md`.
2. Read the relevant entries in `claims/CLAIMS.md`.
3. Search `toolbox/`, `REGRESSION_GATES.md`, and prior `runs/` before opening a new route.
4. Treat `CURRENT_STATE.md` as the current-state authority. File modification time is not authority.

## Research governance
- One main researcher continues the active route unless a genuine mathematical reason requires a branch.
- Reuse stable assets; do not re-prove them without a verification reason.
- Preserve negative results and corrections.
- Do not infer homological return from graph/support recurrence alone.
- Do not replace ordinary Tor/Ext with singular/Tate/stable information without an explicit comparison theorem.
- Do not promote a candidate into `claims/` until a complete proof and normal adversarial audit exist.
- Runs are append-only research records. Do not rewrite old Runs to make history look cleaner.
- Update project memory only after substantive progress: a load-bearing lemma, counterexample, major correction, strict frontier compression, or route change.
- After such a checkpoint: append a Run; update Claims/Toolbox/History only if needed; update `CURRENT_STATE.md` last; then continue research.

## Presentation
Do not spend research budget generating HTML, dashboards, or presentation artifacts. ChatGPT handles the human-facing visualization layer separately.
