## 1. Exact hypotheses and conclusion (C098)

proper set of vertices, f=sum_F e_u, e=1-f, B=eAe. Require

\[
d=\max_{u\in F}\operatorname{pd}_{LEFT} L_u<\infty,
\qquad r=\max_{u\in F}\operatorname{pd}_{RIGHT} R_u<\infty,
\tag{1.1}
\]
\[
\operatorname{Ext}^p_A(L_u,L_v)=0
\quad(u,v\in F,\ p\ge2).
\tag{1.2}
\]

Since d is finite, (1.2) only needs degrees 2<=p<=d. It is a
TWO-ENDPOINT ordinary Ext hypothesis; Ext^1 inside F need NOT vanish.
There is NO physical projective-cover closure condition on F and
NO assumed finite corner-module pd.

### Theorem 1.1

The actual corner modules automatically satisfy

\[
\operatorname{pd}_{B^{op}}Ae\le\max\{r-1,0\},
\qquad \operatorname{pd}_{B}eA\le\max\{d-1,0\}.
\tag{1.3}
\]

For ALL finite LEFT A modules M,Z the natural ordinary Ext restriction

\[
\operatorname{Ext}^n_A(M,Z)\longrightarrow

\[
\operatorname{Tor}^B_n(Re,eM)\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Tor}^A_n(R,M),\qquad n>d+r,
\tag{1.5}
\]

for ALL finite RIGHT R and LEFT M. B is the actual CORNER and the
Tor direction is B->A, not an invented quotient map.

## 2. The finite ordinary Ext window within F

\[
\operatorname{Ext}^p_A(H,K)=0\quad(p\ge2)
\tag{2.1}

the neighbouring terms in every degree p>=2 vanish. This says nothing

The latter need only be <=d by the valid finite-factor pd implication.

Let C be any bounded-above ordinary complex with finite F-cohomology
H_i=H^{-i}C, i>=0. For a fixed F target L_u and n>=1, derived-category
orthogonality removes the cohomology below -n. The finite truncation
in [-n,0] splits into shifted H_i: each connecting map lies in an
Ext group in (2.1) of degree >=2. This is a finite Postnikov splitting,
not a convergence claim about an infinite direct sum.

Consequently

\[
0\longrightarrow\operatorname{Ext}^1_A(H_{n-1},L_u)
\longrightarrow\operatorname{Hom}_{D(A)}(C,L_u[n])
\longrightarrow\operatorname{Hom}_A(H_n,L_u)

\tag{2.2}
\]

by a higher F output. This is the precise difference from C095,
where Ext^2(L_v,L_v)=k repeatedly kills that edge.

Unlike C094, (2.2) is asserted here only for F TARGETS. No pd-one
window for arbitrary Z is claimed.
