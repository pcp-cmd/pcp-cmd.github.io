# 21. Minimal-architecture Sign/Postnikov No-Go Theorem

## Theorem

Consider an ordinary two-vertex realization with:

1. retained primitive \(e\);
2. one deleted primitive \(f\);
3. quotient
   \[
   S=k[t]/(t^2);
   \]
4. a minimal rank-one isolated-second-return resolution
   \[
   0\to eD\xrightarrow b fD\xrightarrow a eD\to S\to0;
   \]
5. one middle lift \(y\in fDf\) satisfying
   \[
   ay=xa,
   \qquad
   yb=\lambda bx;
   \]
6. the minimal Postnikov-activation deformation
   \[
   y^2=\nu ba;
   \]
7. injectivity of \(b\).

Then:

\[
\boxed{
\nu(1-\lambda)=0.
}
\]

Consequently, over characteristic different from \(2\),

\[
\boxed{
\lambda=-1
\Longrightarrow
\nu=0.
}
\]

Hence this entire minimal architecture cannot realize simultaneously:

\[
H_2(K)\cong{}_1S_{\sigma_{-1}}
\]

and a nontrivial Postnikov activation through the unique rank-one homotopy channel.

\(\square\)

This is the B2 result of the run.
