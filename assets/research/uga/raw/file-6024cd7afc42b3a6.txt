## 2. Finite-quotient zero-row window theorem

**Theorem.** If for some a>=0,
\[
e_FP_a=e_FP_{a+1}=\cdots=e_FP_{a+d}=0,
\]
then
\[
\operatorname{pd}_A M\le a+d.
\tag{2.1}
\]

**Proof.** Put X_n=Omega_A^n M and use the actual exact covers
\[
0\longrightarrow X_{n+1}\longrightarrow P_n
\longrightarrow X_n\longrightarrow0.
\tag{2.2}
\]
For n in the displayed interval, e_FP_n=0 implies IP_n=Ae_FP_n=0. Thus P_n is a B module. It is projective over B: A-projectivity lifts maps onto every epimorphism of B modules, since A-linear and B-linear maps between B modules coincide. Equivalently, every actual Ae_x summand with e_FAe_x=0 equals Be_x.

Each X_n in the interval is a quotient of its P_n and hence a B module; X_(a+d+1) is a submodule of P_(a+d) and is also a B module. All maps and ALL kernels in (2.2) are unchanged. The equality J_B=(J_A+I)/I gives J_BP_n=J_AP_n. Every cover in this interval is therefore a minimal B-projective cover, because its actual kernel lies in J_AP_n.

Consequently these are d+1 consecutive covers starting the minimal B-projective resolution of X_a. Since pd_B X_a<=d, its d-th syzygy X_(a+d) is B-projective (or zero). Its minimal cover P_(a+d)->X_(a+d) is an isomorphism, so X_(a+d+1)=0. The actual A resolution terminates there. This proves (2.1). QED.

This proof deletes NO image or differential. It restricts actual covers that are already quotient modules. Neither a homological epimorphism nor a comparison for arbitrary B simples is asserted.
