# T14 — Canonical bimodule syzygy tensor powers do not give ordinary returns

Date: 2026-10-03.

Status: complete self-audited algebra/module counterexample; reuse of the
unchanged actual A5 calibration; no new bounded computation needed;
not independently verified. No novelty claim.
Universal Gap A remains OPEN.

## 1. Why this audit was needed

After T13, a global route was examined through the canonical ordinary
bimodule syzygy M=Omega^1_{A^e}(A). This is available for every elementary
finite-dimensional algebra, unlike a specially chosen radical ideal.
It is projective on both ordinary A sides.

The tempting shortcut was to tensor this actual module repeatedly,
use a positive finite matrix to prove nontermination, and identify
those powers with actual minimal ordinary syzygies. That identification
is false. Projective summands can be introduced on tensoring.
The counterexample below uses actual nonzero tensor powers, not a
mere graph cycle or singular-category assertion.

## 2. Actual first-syzygy columns

Let D(i,j)=dim e_iAe_j and choose the usual minimal bimodule cover

\[
P_0=\bigoplus_i Ae_i\otimes_k e_iA
\overset{\mu}{\longrightarrow}A,\qquad M=\ker\mu.
\tag{2.1}
\]

The map mu is multiplication. It is a projective cover over A^e:
the semisimple bimodule top of A is exactly the diagonal vertex algebra.
Thus M is the actual first minimal bimodule syzygy.

The exact sequence 0->M->P_0->A->0 splits as a left A-module sequence
and as a right A-module sequence, since the respective target A is
projective. Hence M is projective on each side.

For each right column j,

\[
P_0e_j\simeq\bigoplus_i(Ae_i)^{D(i,j)}.
\tag{2.2}
\]

The surjection onto Ae_j splits as a left A-module map. Krull--Schmidt
cancellation gives

\[
Me_j\simeq\bigoplus_i(Ae_i)^{T(i,j)},\qquad T=D-I.
\tag{2.3}
\]

Here T has nonnegative entries. Inductively, the actual ordinary
tensor powers, not a support approximation, satisfy

\[
M^{\otimes_A p}e_j
\simeq\bigoplus_i(Ae_i)^{(T^p)(i,j)}.
\tag{2.4}
\]

Indeed tensoring M with a column Ae_i gives the actual module Me_i.
One-sided projectivity also identifies these ordinary tensor powers
with the derived powers, concentrated in degree zero.

Thus a cycle of T does show that these actual modules do not vanish.
It still does not show an ordinary Tor tail for the original A.

## 3. The unchanged A5 calibration gives an actual counterexample

Rename the old A5 vertices u (the dual-number corner) and w (the
other vertex). Use alpha:u->w and beta:w->u, and basis
e_u,e_w,alpha,beta,t with sole nonzero radical product beta alpha=t.
The other length-two product alpha beta is zero and all products
with t and a radical element are zero.

This is the same actual algebra as C071 / the Primitive-Seed
Correction, with labels renamed. Its global dimension is two.
For calibration only, the literal module identities are

\[
J(Ae_u)\simeq Ae_w,\qquad J(Ae_w)\simeq L_u.
\tag{3.1}
\]

The first isomorphism sends e_w to alpha and beta to t; the second
radical is k beta. Thus pd L_u=1 and pd L_w=2 over every field.
This reuses the existing all-degree proof, not a new computation.

In order (u,w),

\[
D=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
T=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{3.2}
\]

Consequently dim M=8 and the first three actual tensor dimensions
are 8,13,21. Equation (2.4), in particular the actual u-loop,
proves M^{tensor_A p}!=0 for EVERY p>=1. M is projective on both
sides and even has finite A^e-pd, since the regular bimodule has
the existing length-two minimal resolution.

Yet ordinary Tor_n^A(R_z,L_y)=0 for EVERY n>=3 and every simple
pair. This already falsifies a bare inference from actual infinite
canonical-syzygy tensor powers to supported ordinary Tor.

The failure is visible after just two tensors. Put F=M tensor_A -.
Tensor the split-on-the-right sequence (2.1) with L_u. The resulting
ordinary projective cover is Ae_u->L_u, so

\[
F(L_u)\simeq\Omega_A L_u\simeq Ae_w.
\tag{3.3}
\]

Then (2.3) gives

\[
F^2(L_u)\simeq Me_w\simeq Ae_u\ne0,
\quad\text{whereas}\quad \Omega_A^2L_u=0.
\tag{3.4}
\]

All further F iterates of this nonzero projective remain nonzero
projective. They have no positive ordinary Tor with any right
simple. The nontermination is therefore carried by actual
projective summands, not by a missed ordinary tail.

## 4. Exact boundary of the audit

For a general late canonical syzygy M_s=Omega^s_{A^e}(A), tensoring
the minimal bimodule resolution with a simple gives its actual
minimal ordinary resolution: the radical coefficients on the
simple side vanish, and those on the other side remain radical.
In particular M_s tensor_A L_y=Omega_A^s L_y.

Repeating the tensor functor need not preserve minimality. A radical
coefficient on the right can act on M_s with a nonradical matrix
entry between its left projective summands, causing cancellations.
The explicit (3.4) shows that this issue is real.

If M_s J is contained in J M_s, tensoring does preserve radical
maps of left projectives. In the canonical situation this is
equivalent to every ordinary right syzygy Omega^s_{A^{op}} R_i
being semisimple: quotient M_s by its left radical and use the
actual right-simple resolution. This is an existing finite exact
syzygy-system mechanism, not a new general solution. Its general
automatic existence is false, as the unchanged Saorin example
has no positive simple self-Ext but has an infinite supported
off-diagonal tail.

The calibration DOES NOT refute a theorem with additional genuinely
late, all-source, non-infinite-+ hypotheses. In this finite-gldim
example M_s=0 for s>=3. It refutes the shortcut that drops control
of projective summands/minimality, not every possible late-syzygy
argument.

Nor is M a negative layer of the derived quotient of A; none of
C065, C067 or C070's actual-model comparison theorems is contradicted.
The ordinary supported-migration gap in C076 remains unchanged.

## 5. Normal adversarial self-audit

1. M is the kernel of the actual minimal bimodule projective cover,
   not an invented derived residue.
2. Both side projectivities use split ordinary sequences with target A.
3. T=D-I is proved by actual column splittings; (2.4) is an actual
   tensor equality, so nontermination is stronger than graph evidence.
4. The A5 multiplication, simple resolutions and gldim two are reused
   with explicit vertex renaming; no old Run or computation is rerun.
5. The nonzero module in (3.4) is projective, whereas the actual
   second ordinary minimal syzygy is zero.
6. Ordinary Tor conclusions use these actual projective/finite
   resolutions, not stable, singular or Tate information.
7. The semisimple-syzygy criterion is identified with existing assets,
   not promoted as an independent new closure theorem.
8. Extra late all-source hypotheses are not claimed false.
9. The audit is same-author, not independent verification.

## 6. Effect on current research

The current goal is Universal Gap A for arbitrary finite size.
This audit prevents a false global shortcut from canonical
bimodule-syzygy tensor nontermination to ordinary supported returns.
Any continuation of that representation must prove that its
projective summands are controlled in ordinary homology.

The accepted positive frontier stays C076: finite ordinary migration
and global-infinity equivalence, with the physical reverse-support
step still missing. Existing Target A, local-trivial, supported-weight
and general higher-output branches are retained. Further conditions
without a support-preserving bridge, a proved terminating reduction
or a contradiction remain low priority.
