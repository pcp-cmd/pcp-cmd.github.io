## 2. Algebra and the actual projective injection

Work over any field. Let B be the path algebra of

\[
r\xrightarrow{c_r}u\xleftarrow{c_s}s,\qquad u\xrightarrow{a}t.
\]

Products are read right to left. Its nine basis elements are four idempotents, three arrows, and the paths ac_r,ac_s. Its actual LEFT projectives have dimensions

\[
\dim P_r=\dim P_s=3,\quad\dim P_u=2,\quad\dim P_t=1.
\]

The map i:P_u -> P_r direct_sum P_s, e_u |-> (c_r,c_s), a |-> (ac_r,ac_s), is an actual injective radical map. Let Y be its cokernel. Then

\[
0\to P_u\overset{i}{\longrightarrow}P_r\oplus P_s\to Y\to0
\tag{2.1}
\]

is minimal, pd_B Y=1, and Y has one-dimensional components at r,s,u,t. Choose its actions so c_r: k_r -> k_u is 1, c_s: k_s -> k_u is -1, and a:k_u -> k_t is 1. Signs remain valid in characteristic 2.

Put D=k[x]/(x^2), Lambda=B tensor_k D (dimension 18), and W=Y tensor_k k_D. Form the actual one-point extension A with old corner Lambda and new LEFT projective

\[
P_q=ke_q\oplus W,\qquad\operatorname{rad}P_q=W.
\]

The only new Peirce column is W; its old radical actions are the displayed B module actions, and x acts as zero. The old projectives and their actual resolutions are unchanged. Thus A has dimension 23 and five simple labels.

## 3. Full ordinary period-one kernel lemma

For any actual minimal injection i:Q -> P of finite B-projectives, put Y=coker i. Tensor its two-term exact projective resolution with the actual minimal D-resolution of k_D. Since k is a field, the ordinary total complex is exact. Its degree zero is P_D=P tensor D; every positive degree is P_D direct_sum Q_D. The first differential is (x,i), and subsequent differentials have matrix

\[
\begin{pmatrix}x&i\\0&-x\end{pmatrix}.
\]

All entries are radical, so this is a real minimal Lambda-resolution. Let Z=Omega_Lambda(Y tensor k_D)=xP_D+i(Q_D) inside P_D.

Projection onto the first coordinate gives an actual Lambda-isomorphism

\[
\ker(x,i)\cong Z.
\tag{3.1}
\]

Indeed, if xp+i(q)=0, reduction modulo x and injectivity of i imply q=xq_1. Hence p lies in xP_D+i(Q_D). If p=0, injectivity of i gives q=0. Conversely p=xp_1+i(q_1) lifts to (p,-xq_1) in the kernel. The lift is independent of its chosen expression, because the projection is injective. This proves Omega Z=Z using FULL kernels, not Betti repetition.

The real minimal cover of Z is P_D direct_sum Q_D, so its head is top P direct_sum top Q. Apply this to (2.1):

\[
\Omega_A L_q=W,\quad\Omega_A^2 L_q=Z,\quad\Omega_A Z\cong Z,

\[
\operatorname{top}Z=L_r\oplus L_s\oplus L_u,\qquad
[Z]=(1,1,3,3,0),\qquad T(Z)=\{r,s,u\}.
\tag{3.2}
\]

Here T means the labels of ACTUAL recurrent projectives in the ordinary minimal resolution. In particular Z contains L_t as a composition factor, with multiplicity three.
