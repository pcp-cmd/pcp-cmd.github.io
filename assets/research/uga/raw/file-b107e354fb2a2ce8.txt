**Main blocker.** There is no verified construction taking the exact-zero canonical product family, or the finite-pd fifth recurrence, to a nonzero proper idempotent whose corner retains an infinite-pd witness. Support recurrence alone cannot do this (`31c4037065600009`, `78e3ab82c1e9d0e7`, `9110d8cc8257b511`).

**Highest-priority unresolved bridge.** Prove a canonical secondary-corner theorem retaining the actual quotient/kernel/higher-Tor provenance: either a compatible transfer product is nonzero, or the complementary support contains a proper corner with a verified infinite projective resolution.

They do not establish proposition failure; the terminal model obstruction itself descends to a one-vertex infinite-plus corner (`b4f467013c4ed631`).

**Calibration caveat.** Do not read SUBSTANTIAL as “almost solved”: each such component still has an unmatched hypothesis on the actual dead-zone construction.

**Central missing lemma.** Let (A) be a finite-dimensional basic complex algebra with primitive idempotents (e_1,\ldots,e_v), let (f=\sum_{i\in F}e_i) define a nonzero proper vertex-minimal dead-zone corner (B=fAf), and let (R_a^B,L_b^B) be a corner-simple pair with (\operatorname{Tor}_n^B(R_a^B,L_b^B)\ne0) for infinitely many (n) and (e_bAe_a\ne0). Apply the canonical multiplication maps and the tagged comparison of `6eafbbe6a6361a9c`. If the resulting persistent ambient Tor endpoints admit no compatible nonzero product through (f), and if the fifth recurring outside simple has finite projective dimension whenever that branch occurs, then there exists a nonzero proper idempotent (g\le 1-f) (or a canonically specified proper idempotent built from the defect support) such that (gAg) has infinite global dimension, with an explicit infinite minimal resolution witness inherited functorially from the comparison state. The support of (g) must be strictly smaller than the current unresolved support so iteration terminates.

This is non-trivial because a recurring fifth-branch simple may be projective (`31c4037065600009`), a recurrent radical edge may miss the ordered Peirce block (`78e3ab82c1e9d0e7`), and even paired (A/A^{op}) support can have both ordered products zero (`9110d8cc8257b511`).

## §3 Dangerous heuristic lines and strategies not to pursue

**Dangerous heuristic lines**

- “A simple type recurring in infinitely many higher errors supplies a persistent ambient Tor partner.” **Status: Mathematically incorrect as stated.** The recurring type can be projective, so all positive ambient Tor vanishes (`31c4037065600009`). Correct form: first split by ambient projective dimension as in `74cb54be76b478c7`.
- “A recurrent radical edge can be concatenated to the fixed Tor endpoint.” **Status: Not automatic.** Relations can kill or misorient the needed product; `78e3ab82c1e9d0e7` is an explicit obstruction. The correct target is an actual nonzero ordered product as in `ab1afea4df0ce668`.
- “Using both (A) and (A^{op}) removes cancellation.” **Status: Mathematically incorrect as stated.** `9110d8cc8257b511` preserves paired support while both return products vanish. Opposite symmetry helps only with canonical coefficient data.
- “If the leading associated-graded coefficient cancels, one must increase depth.” **Status: Conflated with a stronger claim.** A deeper but nonzero product already closes by `ab1afea4df0ce668`; only exact zero requires descent.
- “Two infinite one-vertex factors in the dumbbell give ambient self-return.” **Status: Not automatic.** `12f95b5af9a6fd16` shows the target simple is mismatched after truncation.

**Strategies not to pursue**

- Do not prove another support-only fifth-branch lemma: `31c4037065600009` already defeats the route.
- Do not infer a nonzero product from two nonzero Peirce spaces: `9110d8cc8257b511` kills both ordered products.
- Do not stop at a recurrent adjacent radical edge: `78e3ab82c1e9d0e7` shows permanent displacement.
- Do not use radical nilpotence before constructing a recurrence-preserving next defect: exact zero is not itself a contradiction.
- Do not relabel a split-corner syzygy as the matching ambient simple: `12f95b5af9a6fd16` records the mismatch.
- Do not draft a final Han proof from Gap A alone: the ordinary-Hochschild bridge remains unverified.
