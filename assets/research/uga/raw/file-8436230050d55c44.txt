**Current best proof skeleton.**

1. Record exact metadata and version history for arXiv:2608.00177v1.
2. Extract the final algebra's explicit construction and prove it is an ordinary finite-dimensional \(\mathbb C\)-algebra.
3. Verify every categorical equivalence used to identify its dg singularity category.
4. Prove that the identified singularity category is nonzero.
5. Deduce \(\operatorname{gldim}(A)=\infty\) from that nonvanishing with all hypotheses matched.
6. Verify the Hochschild-homology comparison between positive-degree \(HH_n(A)\) and the dg singularity category.
7. Prove the relevant dg category is Hochschild-acyclic.
8. Deduce \(HH_n(A)=0\) for every \(n\ge1\).
9. Submit the integrated counterexample theorem to the independent verifier.

**Central missing lemma.** There exists an explicitly defined ordinary finite-dimensional complex algebra \(A\) for which the canonical dg singularity category is both nonzero and Hochschild-acyclic, and the comparison map used in the candidate source identifies this acyclicity with \(HH_n(A)=0\) for all \(n\ge1\). This is non-trivial because the construction passes through geometric, dg, quotient, periodization, and ordinary-algebra interfaces, any one of which can invalidate the final deduction if its hypotheses or enhancement compatibility fail.

**Input required.** An identifiable primary source with stable arXiv identifier, authors, version, date, exact theorem statement, and accessible dependencies.

**Output claimed.** A bounded source package whose claims can be reproduced without relying on search snippets.

**Available facts.** None; bibliographic metadata is not a mathematical fact.

**Missing verification on the actual model.** (1) Download and hash v1; (2) inspect version history; (3) identify all load-bearing citations; (4) search for corrections or conflicting follow-up.

**Failure mode if ignored.** A changed, withdrawn, or incorrectly parsed version could be mistaken for the source actually proved.

### Construction → ordinary finite-dimensional algebra — leave the dg/geometric world correctly

**Output claimed.** A specific associative unital algebra \(A\) of finite \(\mathbb C\)-dimension, concentrated in degree zero.

**Failure mode if ignored.** A dg, topological, or infinite-dimensional counterexample does not answer the stated conjecture.

**Input required.** The precise comparison theorem between \(HH_n(A)\) and Hochschild homology of the canonical dg singularity category, plus a proof that the latter is acyclic.

**Failure mode if ignored.** Vanishing in the wrong grading, for a different enhancement, or only in sufficiently high degrees of a related category may not yield the advertised all-positive-degree vanishing.

# §3 Dangerous heuristic lines and strategies not to pursue

- “The abstract says \(HH_n=0\), so the conjecture is disproved.” **Status: Mathematically incorrect as stated.** An abstract is a claim, not a verifier-accepted proof.
- “A dg counterexample automatically gives an ordinary one.” **Status: Not automatic.** The final ordinary-algebra realization is the main interface to audit.
- “An equivalence of triangulated homotopy categories transports Hochschild homology.” **Status: Conditional only.** Hochschild homology depends on dg/enhancement data; the exact Morita/enhancement statement must be proved.
- “Nonzero \(K_0\) and vanishing Hochschild homology of a phantom automatically survive every quotient and periodization.” **Status: Conditional only.** Compatibility with the exact construction is load-bearing.
- “The source's use of an AI model makes it wrong.” **Status: Mathematically incorrect as stated.** Provenance raises the audit burden but neither proves nor refutes the mathematics.

**Strategies not to pursue.**

- Do not certify the source from its arXiv existence, title, or author assertion.
- Do not replace reproduction with a survey citation or secondary summary.
- Do not conflate the previously known dg analogue with the ordinary conjecture.
- Do not compress the quotient/periodization compatibility into “standard.”
- Do not claim a new contribution merely because Phase I independently found an auxiliary fact.

# §4 Missing bridge lemmas

### Bridge 1 — ordinary-algebra realization

**Target statement.** The candidate source's final object \(A\) is an explicitly defined ordinary associative unital finite-dimensional complex algebra.

**Prerequisites.** Complete definitions of every algebra/category and construction used to produce \(A\).

**Existing facts to use.** None.

**Missing checks.** Reproduce the construction, dimensions, and degree-zero concentration.

**Closure criterion.** A cold reader can reconstruct \(A\) and verify finite-dimensional ordinary-algebra status without trusting the source's conclusion. Independent and parallelizable.

### Bridge 2 — nonzero singularity category

**Target statement.** The canonical dg singularity category of the final algebra \(A\) is nonzero, with an explicit surviving object or invariant, hence \(\operatorname{gldim}(A)=\infty\).

**Prerequisites.** Bridge 1 and every dg quotient/Morita equivalence in the source.

**Existing facts to use.** None.

**Missing checks.** Enhancement compatibility and a nonzeroness witness.

**Closure criterion.** The nonzero object/invariant is transported through a fully verified chain to \(D_{sg}(A)\). Dependent on Bridge 1.

### Bridge 3 — all-positive-degree Hochschild vanishing

**Target statement.** For the same final algebra \(A\), \(HH_n(A)=0\) for every integer \(n\ge1\).

**Prerequisites.** Bridge 1, the canonical dg singularity comparison, and a verified Hochschild-acyclicity computation.

**Existing facts to use.** `04fe02e4d9a47b9f` only supplies an obstruction to naive reasoning, not the target.

**Missing checks.** Degree conventions, comparison theorem hypotheses, Morita/localization compatibility, and low-degree cases.

**Closure criterion.** A verifier accepts the complete comparison-and-acyclicity argument for every positive degree. Dependent on Bridge 1 and parallelizable with Bridge 2 after the construction is fixed.
