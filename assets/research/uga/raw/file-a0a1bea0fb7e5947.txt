# Exact audit of the nine-dimensional screening calibration

Work over Q using fractions.Fraction. A has vertices 0=y, 1=x,
basis e0,e1,u,u2,u3,a,au,b,ub, arrows a:0->1 and b:1->0,
and relations ba=u^2, ab=aub=0. The local y corner is k[u]/u^4.

Check all 729 associativity triples, actual A-linear minimal resolution
maps for both simples, their finite exact periodic certificates, all
four simple-pair ordinary bar complexes in degrees 0..4 with incoming
degree 5, and the induced projection to C=k[u]/u^2 at pair (0,0).

Check the retained minimal A_infinity model H=C+M[1], M having basis
v,w with u v=-w and v u=w, negative products zero, m3(u,u,u)=v.
Check its differential square, Tor and projection through degree 8
with incoming degree 9. These are bounded self-checks, not independent
verification or a substitute for the generic all-degree parity proof.

The all-degree source-y period is four; source-x is period two after
the first syzygy. Both give ordinary visible Tor. This algebra is
infinite +, not a Universal Gap A counterexample.
