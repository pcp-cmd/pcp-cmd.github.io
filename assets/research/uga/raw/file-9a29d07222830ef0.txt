# 18. Singularly trivial augmentation — what it really means

\[

\tag{18.1}

Equivalently, a representative of

\[

This is the correct projective-factorization statement.

One must **not** claim that \(f\) itself necessarily factors through a perfect object.

# 23. Quantum-exterior period-two calibration

Consider the quantum exterior algebra

\[
A_q
=
k\langle x,y\rangle/
(x^2,y^2,xy+qyx).
\]

For the standard two-dimensional left ideals

\[
M_\alpha=A_q(x+\alpha y),
\]

the relation

\[
(x+q\alpha y)(x+\alpha y)=0
\]

gives

\[
\boxed{
\Omega M_\alpha
\simeq
M_{q\alpha}.
}
\tag{23.1}
\]

If \(q\) has order \(2\), for generic nonzero \(\alpha\),

\[
\boxed{
\Omega^2M_\alpha
\simeq
M_\alpha.
}
\tag{23.2}
\]

So \(M_\alpha\) is periodic of period dividing \(2\).

The algebra is local, so \(M_\alpha\) has the unique simple module \(k\) as a quotient.

However the simple \(k\) over the two-generator quantum complete intersection has complexity \(2\), whereas a nonprojective periodic module has complexity \(1\).

Therefore \(k\) is not periodic.

Thus:

\[
\boxed{
\text{periodic module}
+
\text{simple quotient}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{periodic simple}.
}
\tag{23.3}
\]

This directly refutes any orbit-accessibility argument that tries to transfer periodicity itself from \(N\) to a simple anchor.

The calibration is even self-injective, so the failure cannot be blamed on confusing stable and singular categories.

# 26. Finite-stage algebraic anchor loci

Suppose

\[
\{M_\lambda\}_{\lambda\in X}
\]

is an algebraic family of \(D\)-modules of fixed dimension vector over a finite-type parameter variety \(X\).

For a fixed module \(L\):

\[

\]

is the kernel of a matrix whose entries are regular functions of \(\lambda\).

Hence the loci

\[
\{\lambda:
\dim\operatorname{Hom}_D(L,M_\lambda)\ge d\}
\]

are determinantal closed subsets.

For stable Hom, maps factoring through projectives are images of finite composition maps through the finitely many indecomposable projectives.

After a finite rank stratification, the dimension of

\[
\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_D(L,M_\lambda)
\]

is constructible.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\{\lambda:
\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_D(L,M_\lambda)\neq0\}
}
