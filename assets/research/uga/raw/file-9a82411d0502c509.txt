## 1. Actual ordinary quotient maps

A is finite-dimensional elementary over any field; use the left/right
simples, beta_zy(n) and physical D(y,z)=dim e_yAe_z from T16.
I_L(A) and I_R(A) are the two separately defined infinite-pd sets.

For a vertex block S, put e_S=sum_{x in S}e_x and

\[
C_S=A/Ae_SA.
\]

The retained simples have the same labels. The natural ordinary
relative bar map for A -> C_S gives

\[
\epsilon^S_n:\operatorname{Tor}^A_n(R_z,L_y)
\longrightarrow\operatorname{Tor}^{C_S}_n(R_z,L_y),\qquad y,z\notin S.
\tag{1.1}
\]

This map exists without a homological epimorphism or a section.
For nested blocks T subset S it factors, literally on the ordinary
bar complexes, through A -> C_T -> C_S. This is a chain-map statement,
not a comparison inferred from singular/Tate information.

### Hypothesis E_N for one quotient

There is a finite N>=0 such that (1.1) is surjective for EVERY retained
pair and EVERY EVEN n>=N. Neither odd degrees, injectivity, a split
chain map, a section nor a coefficient perfectness hypothesis is part
of this ordinary statement.

It concerns all simple pairs. Supported-pair comparison alone will
not give the finite-pd-label control below.

## 2. High even comparison controls individual simple pds

### Theorem 2.1 — No new infinite labels on either side

Under E_N for A -> C_S,

\[
I_L(C_S)\subseteq I_L(A)\setminus S,\qquad
I_R(C_S)\subseteq I_R(A)\setminus S.
\tag{2.1}
\]

More precisely, if a=pd_A L_y<infinity for y notin S and

\[
m=2\left\lceil\frac{\max(N,a+1)}2\right\rceil,
\]

then

\[
\operatorname{pd}_{C_S}L_y\le m-1.
\tag{2.2}
\]

The direct right bound is identical with a=pd_right,A R_z.

#### Proof

The even integer m is strictly greater than a and at least N.
Every A-Tor_m(R_z,L_y) is zero. For ALL retained z, surjectivity
makes C_S-Tor_m(R_z,L_y) zero. Thus the ENTIRE m-th minimal
C_S-projective term of L_y is zero. A minimal resolution cannot
restart after a zero cover, so pd_C_S L_y<=m-1.

For a finite-pd right R_z, use all retained LEFT tests L_y.
Their vanishing at the same m makes the whole m-th minimal RIGHT
projective term zero. This proves the direct right bound and both
inclusions. No identification of infinite-pd side labels is used. QED.

In particular C062's surjectivity in all even degrees gives a finite
quotient pd bounded by a+1 for every ambient finite-pd simple, even
though it supplies no full Tor isomorphism or algebra section. This
is the missing ordinary no-new-label consequence of that bridge.
No converse of (2.1) is claimed.

## 3. Even comparison also preserves absence of visible return

Reuse C062, Lemma 1.1, whose input is solely the ordinary cover identity.
For any elementary ordinary algebra B, set

\[
b_n(B)=\sum_{y,z}\dim(e_yBe_z)\,
              \dim\operatorname{Tor}^B_n(R_z,L_y),\qquad
t_n(B)=\sum_y\dim e_y\Omega_B^n L_y.
\]

Then b_n=t_n+t_{n+1}, with nonnegative integer summands. If all
sufficiently high EVEN b_n were zero, both parity sequences t_n
would eventually be zero, and then all high b_n would be zero.
Consequently infinite + in B always gives some fixed physically
supported pair in infinitely many EVEN degrees.

### Theorem 3.1 — Supported infinity lifts through E_N

If C_S is infinite + and E_N holds, A is infinite +.

Choose the supported even tail in C_S just described. Discard its
finitely many degrees below N. Surjectivity gives a nonzero ordinary
A class for the SAME retained pair in each remaining degree.
The physical component e_yC_Se_z is a quotient of e_yAe_z, so its
nonzero support lifts too. QED.

Hence a non-infinite-+ A has non-infinite-+ C_S under E_N.
This is stronger than merely producing an infinite ordinary pair
with unproved reverse support. It does not say a preselected
quotient pair must itself have an even tail.

## 4. An actual badness-preserving strict count move

### Theorem 4.1 — Eventual even comparison is an eligible count reduction

Let A be hypothetical bad: gd A=infinity and A is not infinite +.
Choose x in I_L(A), and put C=A/Ae_xA. If this quotient satisfies
E_N, then C is bad and

\[
\boxed{|I_L(C)|\le |I_L(A)|-1,\qquad
       |I_R(C)|\le |I_R(A)|.}
\tag{4.1}
\]

Thus the actual left count strictly decreases, and so does the sum
of the separately chosen left/right counts. The statement does not
require x in I_R(A).

#### Proof

Theorem 3.1 passes absence of infinite + to C.
Reuse the actual-tail result C058 to prove gd C=infinity. To spell
out its ordinary input: high terms P_n(L_x) have e_xP_n=0 by
non-infinite-+, since each reverse-supported pair from L_x has
only finitely many nonzero Tor degrees. Thus they use only Ae_z
with e_xAe_z=0. Each such projective is annihilated by Ae_xA and
is exactly Ce_z. A sufficiently high real X=Omega_A^s L_x has
its SAME minimal resolution over A and C. It has infinite A-pd,
hence infinite C-pd. In particular C is not finite-gldim.

Theorem 2.1 creates no new infinite labels in C on either side.
Since the actually infinite left label x has been removed, the
left inclusion is strict in cardinality, giving (4.1). QED.

For a chosen infinite RIGHT label there is a direct right version.
The high right terms of R_x have Q_n e_x=0 and are sums of e_yA
with e_yAe_x=0. They are annihilated by Ae_xA and equal e_yC,
so the same actual right tail has infinite C-pd. Equation (2.1)
then decreases the right count by at least one and does not
increase the left count. No opposite-infinite-+ theorem is used.

This proves both parts of eligibility: actual badness preservation
AND strict count decrease. Merely erasing an infinite label, or
showing that the quotient is smaller, would prove neither by itself.

Existing bridges supply E_N under their EXACT hypotheses:

- an ordinary corner section (C061) gives all-degree split comparison,
  hence E_0;
- C062's actual negative homology, zero in positive even weights and
  C^e-projective in every odd weight, gives E_0, even for unbounded
  degrees and nonzero higher operations;
- C067's bounded negative ceiling a, actual C^e-pd bound D and actual
  derived tensor nilpotence ell give high same-pair isomorphism for

No such coefficient or section hypothesis is inferred automatically.
These ordinary facts provide a strict count move at a checked

## 5. Proved inheritance without assuming coefficient inheritance

### All-block eventual-even comparison property

For every nonempty proper vertex block S, assume A -> C_S satisfies
E_{N_S} for some finite N_S. The number of blocks is finite. Choose

\[
N=\max_S N_S
\tag{5.1}
\]

(put N=0 for a one-vertex algebra). Call this the all-block hypothesis;
it is a statement about ACTUAL ordinary maps, not an assertion that
every finite algebra has them. A primitive-only comparison hypothesis
does not suffice for the inheritance proof below.

### Lemma 5.1 — The same ordinary hypothesis passes to every block quotient

Let B=C_T. For a block U of retained vertices, its quotient is
D=A/Ae_{T union U}A. For each pair retained in D, natural maps factor

\[
\operatorname{Tor}^A_n\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Tor}^B_n\longrightarrow\operatorname{Tor}^D_n.
\tag{5.2}
\]

The composite is onto for every even n>=N, by the all-block hypothesis
at T union U. Therefore the SECOND map is onto in those degrees.
B has the same all-block property with cutoff at most N. QED.

This proof does not require the first map to be onto, a chain section,
or any inheritance of the negative modules, formality, projectivity,
perfectness or tensor nilpotence. It proves the required ordinary-map
inheritance directly. An available coefficient model is one way to
certify an original comparison, not the carrier of the induction.

### Theorem 5.2 — Whole-class closure at every finite size

Every elementary finite-dimensional algebra satisfying the all-block
eventual-even comparison hypothesis satisfies Universal Gap A.

Suppose A in this class were bad. If either infinite-simple count
were at most two, C080 would already contradict badness. Otherwise
choose an infinite LEFT simple x and apply Theorem 4.1. It produces
a bad quotient with strictly lower left count, and Lemma 5.1 preserves
the all-block property. Repeat on the actual current quotient.

At every step eligibility is proved, no new infinite labels occur,
and the left count drops by at least one. After at most
|I_L(A)|-2 steps a bad algebra with at most two infinite left simples
would remain, contradicting C080. Thus no bad A in this class exists.
The direct right selection gives the corresponding bound
|I_R(A)|-2 on a right-chosen chain. QED.

The theorem permits different original block comparisons to be
certified by different bridge mechanisms. It does not claim that
primitive C062/C067 hypotheses automatically hold for every block,
or that the all-block class contains the entire C061 infinite-vertex-
only class. The latter already has its own proved section inheritance.

An explicit tower of checked E_N primitive moves also terminates by
the same count argument over a C080 terminal algebra, even without
the all-block assumption. That version still requires checking each
actual next move; it is not an automatic-existence theorem.

## 6. Uniform bound for this class under all-degree supported vanishing

### Theorem 6.1

Assume the all-block comparison cutoff N and suppose ALL reverse-
supported A simple-pair Tor vanishes for EVERY n>=M>=1. Let

\[
M_* = 2\left\lceil\frac{\max(M,N)}2\right\rceil.
\tag{6.1}
\]

If A has v vertices, then

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{gldim}A\le(v-1)M_*.}
\tag{6.2}
\]

#### Proof

For every block quotient B=C_S, a B-supported pair is physically
A-supported. At all even n>=M_* its A-Tor is zero and the comparison
is onto. Thus b_n(B)=0 for every such even degree. The nonnegative
identity b_n=t_n+t_{n+1} makes t_n(B)=0 for ALL n>=M_* and then
b_n(B)=0 for ALL n>=M_*. Every supported B pair vanishes in this
full range, since it is a nonnegative summand of b_n(B).

Now induct on the number of vertices, using actual resolutions.
With one vertex, a nonsemisimple finite local algebra has a
nonterminating minimal free simple resolution and nonzero supported
diagonal Tor in every degree. The assumed vanishing therefore makes
that endpoint semisimple, with gd zero.

For an arbitrary vertex x of the current algebra B, all terms in
the minimal resolution of L_x from degree M_* onward have zero x component.
As in Theorem 4.1, the real X=Omega_B^{M_*} L_x has the SAME minimal
resolution over the ordinary quotient D=B/Be_xB. This comparison
does not require L_x to have infinite pd; zero X is allowed.
By Lemma 5.1 and the preceding support-vanishing argument, D has
both the all-block comparison property and the cutoff M_*.
The inductive bound is gd D<=(number_of_vertices(B)-2)M_*.
Ordinary dimension shifting gives pd_B L_x<=M_*+gd D.
This holds for EVERY x, proving (6.2). QED.

The bound retains the actual N and the EVERY-pair/EVERY-degree
vanishing hypothesis. It is not a universal excursion/page bound,
a bound for arbitrary A, or a short finite-window test. Even-only
information is promoted to the full supported range only through
the proved nonnegative ordinary cover identity.
