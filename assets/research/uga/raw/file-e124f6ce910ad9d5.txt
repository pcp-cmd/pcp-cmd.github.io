## 1. C112 — The full actual head-relation kernel supplies a correct bridge

Call a nonzero module primitive-cyclic if it is a quotient of ONE
primitive left projective Ae_t. This includes every simple and permits
nonsemisimple modules and infinitely many isomorphism types. No stable
or derived quotient is meant.

For any finite LEFT module Y, choose a REAL basis of its semisimple
head and homogeneous primitive lifts u_alpha=e_(r_alpha)u_alpha.
Put C_alpha=A u_alpha subset Y, an actual primitive-cyclic submodule.
The lifts minimally generate Y. Each C_alpha has head L_(r_alpha),
and the actual sum map defines a real short exact sequence

\[
0\to F(Y)\to H(Y):=\bigoplus_\alpha C_\alpha
 \overset{\sigma}{\longrightarrow}Y\to0,
\qquad\sigma((x_\alpha))=\sum_\alpha x_\alpha.
\tag{1.1}
\]

F(Y) is the familiar complete relation module of these actual images.
It is not one differential image, one pairwise intersection, or an
assumed direct-sum complement. The induced head map is an isomorphism,
so

\[
F(Y)\subseteq JH(Y).
\tag{1.2}
\]

### Finite relation kernel

If pd LEFT F(Y)=d<infinity, the actual ordinary LES gives, for EVERY
right module R and EVERY r>d+1,

\[
\operatorname{Tor}_r^A(R,H(Y))
 \overset{\sim}{\longrightarrow}\operatorname{Tor}_r^A(R,Y).
\tag{1.3}
\]

Thus sufficiently high ordinary Tor is exactly the sum of the cyclic
pieces' Tor. If Y has infinite pd, at least one C_alpha has infinite
pd, since finite pd of both F and all C_alpha would imply finite pd Y.

For a finite module X write T(X) for its ACTUAL recurrent projective
detector labels: q in T(X) iff Tor_n(R_q,X) is nonzero infinitely often.
Equation(1.3) yields

\[
T(Y)=\bigcup_\alpha T(C_\alpha).
\tag{1.4}
\]

This is all-detector ORDINARY recurrence, not image-factor support.
There is no assertion that erasing images preserves exactness of an
infinite differential complex. The short exact sequence is the proof.

### Ordinary supported return from a primitive-cyclic head detector

If C is a quotient of Ae_r and r in T(C), then A already has a fixed
ordinary supported simple pair. Every composition factor L_j of C
occurs in Ae_r, so D(j,r)=dim e_jAe_r is positive. If all such ordinary
Tor(R_r,L_j) vanished eventually, finitely many factors and the ordinary
LES of a composition filtration would make Tor(R_r,C) vanish eventually.
Contradiction. Some FIXED j therefore has Tor_n(R_r,L_j) nonzero
infinitely often with D(j,r)>0. It may be OFF-DIAGONAL.

This reuses the project's ordinary projective-carrier/devissage bridge.
Under ambient BADNESS, it proves

\[
r\notin T(C)\qquad(C\text{ primitive-cyclic with head }L_r).
\tag{1.5}
\]

No relation between LEFT and RIGHT infinite labels is assumed.

## 2. A genuine arbitrary-size strict carrier reduction

Start with an infinite-pd primitive-cyclic source X and its ORIGINAL
actual minimal resolution. Because there are finitely many projective
labels, sufficiently late syzygies Y=Omega^n X have every projective
head label r_alpha in T(X): all nonrecurrent labels have stopped.
Also T(Y)=T(X) by ordinary projective dimension shifting.

Suppose such a late Y admits a choice of genuine head lifts with
finite-pd full relation kernel F(Y). Equations(1.3)-(1.4) select an
infinite-pd C_alpha with

\[
\varnothing\ne T(C_\alpha)\subseteq T(X).
\tag{2.1}
\]

Its own head r_alpha lies in T(X). Either r_alpha is recurrent in
C_alpha, in which case Section1 gives ordinary SUPPORTED return,
or it is absent from T(C_alpha). In the latter case

\[
\varnothing\ne T(C_\alpha)\subsetneq T(X).
\tag{2.2}
\]

Under badness the first alternative is impossible. Passing from X to
C_alpha changes only the module source on SAME A, retains infinite pd
and a genuine ordinary tail, and strictly lowers |T|. The new source is
again primitive-cyclic. No projective-cover expansion has been ignored:
its whole ordinary resolution is what defines T(C_alpha).

### Whole-class closure

Assume EVERY infinite-pd primitive-cyclic module X has ARBITRARILY LATE
actual syzygies admitting minimal head lifts with finite-pd full relation
kernel F(Y). Then Universal Gap A holds for this whole arbitrary-size,
arbitrary-Loewy class, with a possibly OFF-DIAGONAL ordinary simple pair.

Proof: gd infinity supplies an infinite simple, hence an infinite
primitive-cyclic source. Under badness choose a source with minimum
nonempty |T| among ALL infinite primitive-cyclic modules. This minimum
exists among finitely many subsets of the N projective labels, not
because there are finitely many modules or isomorphism states.
Choose an eligible Y beyond all nonrecurrent projective heads.
Equation(2.2) yields another infinite primitive-cyclic source with a
strictly smaller nonempty |T|. Contradiction. An iterative version has
at most |T(X)|-1 successful strict source moves.

Only the error pd at the chosen event needs to be finite. Values over
different modules/indices can be unbounded. No uniform all-index Tor
comparison or numerical pd bound is claimed. This is a new proper
ordinary finite-error elimination criterion and closing mechanism,
not a theorem that arbitrary finite-pd map images can be erased.

The same proof applies to a designated source family of primitive-cyclic
modules if it contains an infinite source and the eligible cyclic images
remain in that family. Actual syzygy systems that decompose into such
primitive-cyclic states have F=0 on those direct-sum decompositions;
the older single-head FINITE-system theorem is reused and its finite
state restriction is unnecessary for this argument. No blanket
syzygy-finiteness or closure of an arbitrary chosen family is inferred.

For example elementary Nakayama algebras have uniserial primitive
projectives/quotients and uniserial actual cover kernels; nonzero

a reuse/corollary, not a novelty or next-size classification claim.
N7's actual permanent cyclic tail is handled by the ordinary
OFF-DIAGONAL head detector of Section1, retaining C105's witness.

The direct RIGHT proof uses cyclic submodules u_alpha A generated by
primitive right head lifts, RIGHT finite pd of the relation kernel and
ordinary Tor with all LEFT modules. A recurrent left detector equal to
the right cyclic head selects supported Tor by right composition
devissage. It is not the quarantined opposite-infinite-plus transfer.

## 3. When every cyclic image is finite-pd, the REAL relation kernel descends in Loewy length

There is a second correct ordinary move in the SAME real sequence(1.1).
Assume instead every C_alpha has finite LEFT pd, h=max pd C_alpha,
while Y has infinite LEFT pd. Then F(Y) has infinite LEFT pd, and for
every R and r>h the ordinary LES gives

\[
\operatorname{Tor}_{r+1}^A(R,Y)
 \cong\operatorname{Tor}_r^A(R,F(Y)),\qquad
T(F(Y))=T(Y).
\tag{3.1}
\]

Every C_alpha is a submodule of Y. Equation(1.2) implies

\[
\operatorname{Loewy}(F(Y))
 \le\operatorname{Loewy}(Y)-1.
\tag{3.2}
\]

Thus replacing Y by the FULL actual relation kernel retains the
ordinary infinite tail and strictly decreases its positive Loewy
length. It is a valid finite descent for CONSECUTIVE applications of
this same all-images-finite module move. It cannot continue at a
nonzero semisimple infinite-pd module, whose head images are the
infinite-pd simples themselves.

This is NOT a proof that Loewy decreases throughout arbitrary syzygy
iteration: taking new projective covers/syzygies can increase Loewy.
Nor is F necessarily primitive-cyclic, or its new head automatically
recurrent. Do not stitch(3.2) to Section2 without proving those
additional eligibility conditions. The mixed case—both an infinite
cyclic image and an infinite full relation kernel—still lacks the
ordinary carrier comparison(1.4). It remains a real global obstruction.

## 4. Sequential intersections suffice; pairwise intersections do NOT

For a chosen ordering of the actual cyclic images, put
U_m=sum_(alpha<=m) C_alpha and let F_m be the kernel of
direct sum_(alpha<=m) C_alpha -> U_m. Projection to the last summand
gives the REAL exact module sequence

\[
0\to F_{m-1}\to F_m\to C_m\cap U_{m-1}\to0.
\tag{4.1}
\]

F_1=0. If EVERY sequential intersection C_m intersect(sum_previous C)
has finite pd, then the FULL F(Y) has finite pd, with bound at most
the maximum of those finitely many intersection pds. This is a proved
ordinary eligibility test for Section2. Pairwise finite-pd intersections
do not imply the sequential condition, as the actual counterexample
below proves. Conversely finite pd of F alone does not automatically
pass to its submodules/quotients F_m or the individual intersections.

## 5. C113 — Projective images and zero pairwise intersections can still have infinite-pd full relations

For p>=3 over Q, use vertices v,r_1,...,r_p. Take arrows
c_i:v->r_i,a_i:r_i->v and two independent radical-square elements
d_0,d_1 in(v,v). The ONLY nonzero radical products are

\[
a_i c_i=w_{i0}d_0+w_{i1}d_1,
\tag{5.1}
\]

where the vectors w_i in Q^2 span Q^2 and are pairwise nonparallel.
For instance use(1,0),(0,1), then(1,j),1<=j<=p-2. All other radical
products vanish. This gives an associative elementary algebra of
dimension3p+3 and J^3=0. Associativity follows because every nonzero
radical product lies in the annihilated span(d_0,d_1).

Actual LEFT projectives have

\[
P_{r_i}=\langle e_{r_i},a_i\rangle,\qquad
P_v=\langle e_v,c_1,...,c_p,d_0,d_1\rangle.
\tag{5.2}
\]

The real first syzygy Y=Omega L_v=JP_v has head sum_i L_(r_i),
radical L_v^2, and a_i c_i=w_i. Each actual head-generated image
C_i=A c_i has basis(c_i,w_i) and is isomorphic to PROJECTIVE P_(r_i).
Distinct C_i have different heads, and their radical lines are
nonparallel, so

\[
\operatorname{pd}C_i=0,\qquad C_i\cap C_j=0\quad(i\ne j).
\tag{5.3}
\]

Nonetheless the genuine minimal cover/direct image sum
direct sum_i P_(r_i)->Y has kernel

\[
F(Y)=L_v^{p-2}.
\tag{5.4}
\]

The head map is an isomorphism and the radical map is the rank-two
matrix with columns w_i, giving its p-2-dimensional semisimple kernel.
These are actual maps, not a numerical incidence model. In particular

\[
\Omega L_v=Y,\qquad\Omega Y=L_v^{p-2},\qquad
\Omega^{2m}L_v=L_v^{(p-2)^m}\quad(m\ge1).
\tag{5.5}
\]

All covers are genuine minimal covers. This proves pd L_v infinity,
pd F(Y) infinity, and ordinary diagonal Tor_(2m)(R_v,L_v) of dimension
(p-2)^m for EVERY m>=1. Thus the example is already infinite-plus and
is NOT a Universal Gap A counterexample. The actual relation obstruction
reappears in odd syzygy stages; it is not an isolated arbitrary module.

With the first three w_i=(1,0),(0,1),(1,1), the first two cyclic images
have direct projective sum, but
C_3 intersect(C_1+C_2)=J C_3=L_v of infinite pd. It is invisible to
EVERY pairwise intersection test. This breaks the proposed replacement
of the FULL relation test by individual finite-pd images or pairwise
finite-pd/zero cross intersections, in actual ordinary minimal tails.

It also calibrates Section3 positively: all image modules are projective,
so the correct object to keep is the infinite-pd relation kernel, with
