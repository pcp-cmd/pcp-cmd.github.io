## 2. The actual ordinary tensor resolution (C096)

\[
T_n=\bigoplus_{i+j=n}P_i\otimes_k Q_j,
\quad d(p\otimes q)=d_Pp\otimes q+(-1)^i p\otimes d_Qq.
\tag{2.1}
\]

No finite-pd image is deleted and no stable resolution is substituted.

Tensoring with a RIGHT tensor simple kills all differentials. Hence

\[
\operatorname{Tor}^{A}_n(R_z\otimes R_w,L_y\otimes L_v)
\cong\bigoplus_{i+j=n}

\[

\tag{2.3}

\[
