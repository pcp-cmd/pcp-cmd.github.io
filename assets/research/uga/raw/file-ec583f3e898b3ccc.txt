The KLS literature construction indicates proposition failure for the original Han conjecture, conditional here on the still-unclosed integrated dependency chain.

**Current best proof skeleton**

1. Assume a vertex-minimal finite-dimensional bound-quiver algebra (A) has infinite global dimension but is not infinite ((+)).
2. Choose an infinite-projective-dimension simple (S_x) and prove the high syzygy top is eventually supported in the Peirce dead zone (Z(x)=\{y:e_xAe_y=0\}).
3. Prove that applying the idempotent (f_x=\sum_{y\in Z(x)}e_y) to the actual minimal tail gives an infinite minimal resolution over Δ_x=f_xAf_x.
4. By vertex minimality, infer that Δ_x is infinite ((+)).
5. Represent a corner witness by persistent simple Tor/Ext data and compare its corner resolution with the ambient resolution using the exact idempotent error complex.
6. If the error is eventually zero, lift the witness to (A).
7. If the error persists, extract recurrent simple composition factors from cross-Tor and use Peirce compatibility to build an ambient witness.
8. Use the same argument for (A^{op}) to exclude permanent left-right multi-top displacement.
9. Contradict the assumption that (A) is not infinite ((+)).

**Central missing lemma.** Let (A=kQ/I) be a finite-dimensional basic algebra that is vertex-minimal among algebras of infinite global dimension which are not infinite ((+)). Let (xin Q_0) have πd_A S_x=∞, let (Z(x)=\{y\in Q_0:e_xAe_y=0\}), let (f=\sum_{y\in Z(x)}e_y), and let Δ=fAf. Suppose Δ has vertices (u,v) with (e_vΔe_u\neq0) and (\operatorname{Tor}^{Δ}_n(k_u,{}_vk)\neq0) for infinitely many (n). Then either there are vertices (a,bin Q_0) with (e_bAe_a\neq0) and (\operatorname{Tor}^{A}_n(k_a,{}_bk)\neq0) for infinitely many (n), or the idempotent comparison error contains simple composition factors that yield such a pair. This is non-trivial because corner restriction need not be an eventual homological isomorphism and persistent matrix-level cross-Tor may undergo multi-top displacement.

# §3 Dangerous heuristic lines and strategies not to pursue

**Dangerous heuristic lines**

- “A corner is infinite ((+)), so its witness automatically lifts.” **Status: Mathematically incorrect as stated.** Corner restriction is not automatically an eventual homological isomorphism; the comparison requires cross-module hypotheses. Correct form: prove a witness-or-error-witness dichotomy.
- “A periodic module gives a recurrent simple self-Ext witness.” **Status: Not automatic.** Periodicity is a module-level stable statement and does not select a Peirce-compatible simple pair. The verified decoupling example `04fe02e4d9a47b9f` warns that homological persistence and the desired Tor visibility are different obligations.
- “The singularity-category Hochschild calculation already proves the KLS algebra theorem.” **Status: Conditional only.** `bd597721f09fbbf2` closes the formal implication only after all dg and comparison hypotheses are matched.
- “Finite diagonal bimodule dimension might coexist with infinite global dimension.” **Status: Mathematically incorrect as stated.** `0d5910abd98b476c` shows the diagonal bound propagates to all simple left modules.
- “Support graphs alone eliminate cancellation.” **Status: Method shortcut, not a proof step.** A minimal differential gives adjacent radical incidence, but (d^2=0) allows sums of matrix products to cancel; a rank-one, graded, or bilateral extraction theorem is still needed.

**Strategies not to pursue**

- Do not restart unrestricted small-quiver search: first impose the handoff's structural filters and verify them.
- Do not reuse truncated polynomial algebras: `341cd214da0de5ac` excludes them.
- Do not use the dual-number triangular family as a vanishing mechanism: `f57bf3108a82a0e6` excludes it.
- Do not replace the diagonal bimodule by an arbitrary self-Tor-acyclic bimodule: `04fe02e4d9a47b9f` shows only a mismatch prototype.
- Do not import the handoff's low-vertex or dead-zone results as facts before fresh verifier acceptance.
- Do not spend the main Gap A effort recomputing the KLS terminal detector unless a universal bridge explicitly depends on it.
