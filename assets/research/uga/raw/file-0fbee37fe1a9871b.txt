## 2. Vanishing for actual right subideals

Let I be an actual TWO-SIDED ideal with LEFT pd I at most 1. For every RIGHT ideal U contained in A, the actual RIGHT sequence

$$
0\longrightarrow U\longrightarrow A\longrightarrow A/U\longrightarrow0
$$

and the SECOND LEFT factor I give, for every n at least 1,

$$
\operatorname{Tor}^A_n(U,I)
\cong\operatorname{Tor}^A_{n+1}(A/U,I)=0.
\tag{2.1}
$$

This statement requires no LEFT module structure on a mere right ideal U. To discuss LEFT pd of the resulting tensor, now assume U is TWO-SIDED and LEFT pd U=d is finite.

Choose an actual finite LEFT projective resolution

$$
0\longrightarrow P_1\longrightarrow P_0\longrightarrow I\longrightarrow0.
$$

Tensor the FIRST RIGHT U with this resolution. Equation (2.1) gives the genuine LEFT exact sequence

$$
0\longrightarrow U\otimes_A P_1\longrightarrow U\otimes_A P_0
\longrightarrow U\otimes_A I\longrightarrow0.
\tag{2.2}
$$

Each term U tensor P_i is a direct sum of actual LEFT summands Ue_f of U. Their LEFT pds are at most d. Consequently

$$
\operatorname{pd}_{\mathrm{LEFT}}(U\otimes_A I)\le d+1.
\tag{2.3}
$$

In particular the ordinary module

$$
T=I\otimes_A I
$$

has LEFT pd at most 2. This uses actual Tor1 vanishing; it is stronger than merely saying a derived tensor complex is perfect. Neither flat multiplication nor ordinary tensor nilpotence follows.

\operatorname{Ext}^n_A(E,Z)
