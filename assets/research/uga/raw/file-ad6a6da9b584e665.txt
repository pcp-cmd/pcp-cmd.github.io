# T15 — Ordinary closure when only one simple has infinite projective dimension

Date: 2026-10-03.

Status: complete self-audited all-size ordinary proof; standard finite-length
projective-dimension devissage and existing project syzygy identities;
no novelty claim and no new computation; not independently verified.
Universal Gap A remains OPEN in general.

## 1. A global branch, not an added derived invariant

The acceptance target remains Universal Gap A for every finite size.
After T13's supported-migration gap and T14's canonical-tensor audit,
we inspected a global parameter: the actual number of ordinary simples
of infinite projective dimension, on each chosen side separately.

The entire branch with at most one infinite-pd left simple closes.
So does the right version, by an independent direct ordinary argument
on right resolutions. This does not require ideal existence, sections,
formality, a bounded screening page, an Ext generation assumption or
an opposite-side infinite-+ transfer.

The proof is a synthesis of standard composition-series pd bounds and
the project's existing syzygy-component / supported-Tor identity.
It is not a new claim about stable or singular categories.

Let A be elementary finite-dimensional over any field. Write L_x for
left simples, R_x for right simples, and J=rad A. Let P_n(L_x)
be its actual minimal left resolution. Its multiplicity of Ae_z is
dim Tor_n^A(R_z,L_x). Supported means e_xAe_z!=0.

## 2. The finite-length input

If all composition factors of a finite module X have pd<=d on one
stated side, then pd X<=d. Induct on a composition series and use the
ordinary long exact Tor sequence, or the ordinary pd inequality for
a short exact sequence.

This is an upper bound, not its false converse: a finite-pd projective
can have composition factors of infinite pd. Only the proved direction
is used below.

For an elementary algebra, the multiplicity of L_x as a composition
factor is dim e_xX; on the right it is dim X e_x.

## 3. Exactly one infinite-pd left simple gives visible Tor in every degree

### Theorem 3.1

Suppose L_x is the only infinite-pd left simple. Then for EVERY n>=0,

\[
\sum_z \dim(e_xAe_z)\,
       \dim\operatorname{Tor}_n^A(R_z,L_x)
=\dim e_x\Omega_A^nL_x+\dim e_x\Omega_A^{n+1}L_x
\ge2.
\tag{3.1}
\]

Consequently one fixed z with e_xAe_z!=0 has ordinary Tor_n^A(R_z,L_x)
nonzero in infinitely many degrees. A is infinite +, for arbitrary
finite vertex number.

#### Proof

Every actual Omega_A^n L_x has infinite pd. Otherwise dimension shifting
through the first n finite projective terms would give finite pd L_x.

All other left simples have finite pd. There are finitely many, so their
maximum d is finite (put d=0 if there are none). If some Omega_A^n L_x
had no L_x composition factor, Section 2 would give pd<=d, a contradiction.
Therefore dim e_x Omega_A^n L_x>=1 for every n>=0.

Apply the exact component functor e_x(-) to

\[
0\longrightarrow\Omega_A^{n+1}L_x\longrightarrow P_n(L_x)
\longrightarrow\Omega_A^nL_x\longrightarrow0.
\tag{3.2}
\]

The two syzygy components have dimension at least one. Minimality
identifies the left-projective multiplicities in P_n with ordinary
simple-pair Tor, giving (3.1). Its summands have nonnegative dimensions.
For every n at least one supported label z occurs; a finite set of
labels contains one occurring in infinitely many degrees. QED.

The fixed pair need not be diagonal, and the theorem does not claim
one fixed pair is nonzero in every degree.

In particular dim e_xAe_x>=2, by n=0. Thus a unique infinite-pd left
simple cannot have a trivial local corner. This is a consequence of the
actual projective cover, not an inference from quiver cycles.

### Corollary 3.2 — Full branch exclusion

Every finite-dimensional elementary algebra with at most one infinite-pd
left simple satisfies Universal Gap A. If there are none, all simples
have finite pd and the global dimension is finite. If there is one,
Theorem 3.1 gives the required ordinary supported witness.

## 4. Direct ordinary right-side theorem

Suppose R_x is the only infinite-pd RIGHT simple. Use its actual minimal
right resolution Q_n(R_x), with multiplicity of e_yA equal to
dim Tor_n^A(R_x,L_y).

Every Omega_right^n R_x has infinite right pd. By the same composition
series argument it contains R_x in every degree. Apply (-)e_x to its
short exact projective-cover sequence. This gives

\[
\sum_y\dim(e_yAe_x)\,
      \dim\operatorname{Tor}_n^A(R_x,L_y)
=\dim(\Omega_{\rm right}^nR_x)e_x
 +\dim(\Omega_{\rm right}^{n+1}R_x)e_x
\ge2
\tag{4.1}
\]

for every n>=0. Select a fixed y from the finite label set. The
nonzero physical Peirce component is exactly e_yAe_x, the support
required for that SAME ordinary pair (R_x,L_y).

Hence the at-most-one-infinite-pd right-simple branch also satisfies
Gap A. No global opposite-infinite-+ theorem is invoked or cleared.
Ordinary right resolutions compute the stated ordinary A-Tor directly.

## 5. A quantitative termination statement

### Theorem 5.1

Fix a left simple L_x and assume pd L_y<=d<infinity for every y!=x.
Suppose for M>=1,

\[
\operatorname{Tor}_n^A(R_z,L_x)=0
\quad\text{for every }n\ge M\text{ and every }z
\text{ with }e_xAe_z\ne0.
\tag{5.1}
\]

Then

\[
\operatorname{pd}_A L_x\le M+d,\qquad
\operatorname{gldim} A\le M+d.
\tag{5.2}
\]

#### Proof

The hypothesis makes e_xP_M(L_x)=0. Since P_M surjects onto
Omega_A^M L_x, its x component is zero too. All composition factors
of that actual syzygy have pd<=d, so it has pd<=d.
Dimension shifting gives pd L_x<=M+d. Every other simple already
has pd<=d, giving the global bound. QED.

There is a direct right version: fix R_x, bound all other right-simple
pds by d, and assume all ordinary pairs (R_x,L_y) with e_yAe_x!=0
vanish in every n>=M. Then all right simples have pd<=M+d, so all
ordinary simple-pair Tor vanishes above M+d. Minimal left resolutions
then give the same global-dimension bound, without opposite-+ transfer.

The theorem keeps the finite pd bound d and all-degree vanishing.
It is not a uniform finite-window test or a bound depending only on
the number of vertices.

## 6. Existing diagonal-free calibration

Reuse the unchanged Saorin seven-dimensional example from
toolbox/Imported-Outputs/Finite-Syzygy-System-Theorem.md, Section 1.
It has only L_0 of infinite left pd, L_1 of pd one, and

\[
\Omega L_0=M,\quad\Omega M\simeq M,\quad
\operatorname{top}M=L_1,\quad\operatorname{rad}M=L_0.
\tag{6.1}
\]

Thus e_0 Omega^n L_0 has dimension one in every n>=1, while all
positive simple self-Ext vanishes. The actual supported off-diagonal
pair has Tor_n(R_1,L_0)=k for every n>=1 and dim e_0Ae_1=2,
exactly realizing the left-hand side of (3.1) as two.

This confirms that composition return is enough for Gap A and does
not imply top return at the same simple. The existing connected
2N+3-dimensional extension family adds projective sink simples;
L_0 remains the unique infinite-pd left simple, so this calibration
already extends to arbitrary finite vertex number.

No multiplication table, computation or old proof was rerun. The
all-degree ordinary module identities are the reused certificate.
