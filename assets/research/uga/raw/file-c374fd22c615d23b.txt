# T20 — Closed finite-pd blocks give exact ordinary comparison and genuine count descent

Date: 2026-10-04.

Status: complete ordinary proofs, normal adversarial self-audit;
unchanged T18 exact certificates and T19 literal module calibration
reused; no new computation, no novelty claim; not independently verified.
Universal Gap A remains OPEN for arbitrary algebras.

This continues T19's actual finite-factor cross-map problem. Bounded
pd of an image does not erase an ordinary differential. The present
step instead constructs a legitimate ordinary deletion when finite-pd
vertices form an actual projective-cover-closed block. It proves the
same-pair Tor comparison, global-infinity transfer, exact retained
infinite labels, and the precise strictly decreasing count. It also
strengthens T19's common-three-label closure to one-sided containment.

The latest inheritance counterexample admits this alternative block
deletion: its whole right-finite old block can be removed at once,
avoiding newly infinite retained labels and lowering the left count
3->1. This is not a proof that such a block always exists in a bad A.

## 1. Conventions and ordinary inputs

A is finite-dimensional elementary over an arbitrary field k.
I_L and I_R are the separately defined left/right infinite-pd simple
label sets. H_zy records ordinary Tor persistence; W_z and T_y are
its persistent rows and columns. Physical reverse support is
D(y,z)=dim e_yAe_z for Tor(R_z,L_y).

Reuse only complete ordinary interfaces: C080's singleton-detector
and two-source closure, actual Peirce-block resolutions, finite-length
Tor detection, and finite-factor pd bounds. No singular/Tate/stable
information, graph-cycle return, or opposite-infinite-+ theorem enters.

## 2. Containment suffices at three sources

### Theorem 2.1 — Three left infinite labels contained in the right set force visible ordinary Tor

If

\[
|I_L(A)|=3,\qquad I_L(A)\subseteq I_R(A),
\tag{2.1}
\]

then A is infinite +, regardless of total vertex number, socle
separation, or how many EXTRA right infinite labels exist.

#### Proof

Assume A bad and write I=I_L. For each z in I, containment makes
R_z infinite-pd, so W_z is nonempty. The ordinary singleton-detector
bridge would already give visible Tor; under badness |W_z|>=2.
Every W_z lies in I, and misses z since D(z,z)>0. Thus

\[
W_z=I\setminus\{z\}\quad(z\in I).
\tag{2.2}
\]

No visible persistent Tor then forces

\[
e_jAe_z=0\quad\text{for distinct }j,z\in I.
\tag{2.3}
\]

Take ANY infinite-pd right simple R_t. Its sufficiently high actual
minimal right resolution uses only projectives e_jA, j in W_t,
and W_t is contained in I. By (2.3) all different projective types
have Hom zero in BOTH directions. Therefore its actual high tail
is block diagonal, exact component by component, and minimal.

Choose a recurrent label j and augment its genuine one-type block
by the actual right module X=coker(Q_{s+1}^{j}->Q_s^{j}). Ordinary
Tor_n(X,L_j) is nonzero infinitely often. Since X is a quotient
of a sum of e_jA, every right composition factor R_q of X has
e_jAe_q nonzero. Ordinary finite-length detection gives

\[
\dim\operatorname{Tor}_n(X,L_j)
\le\sum_q[X:R_q]\dim\operatorname{Tor}_n(R_q,L_j).
\tag{2.4}
\]

Some fixed q therefore has persistent Tor(R_q,L_j) and its physical
support e_jAe_q!=0. This contradicts badness. Recurrence came from
the actual minimal right block, not from (2.3) or a graph cycle. QED.

### Theorem 2.2 — Direct right containment version

If |I_R|=3 and I_R is contained in I_L, then A is infinite +.

For y in I_R, containment gives an infinite LEFT L_y. Its persistent
column T_y lies in I_R, has at least two labels under badness, and
misses y by diagonal support. It is I_R minus {y}; physical
off-diagonal entries among I_R are zero. Every actual high LEFT
resolution therefore splits by projective type Ae_z, z in I_R.
In a recurrent block its real left module X has Tor(R_z,X) persistent;
all factors L_q have e_qAe_z!=0. Direct LEFT factor detection gives
the fixed supported pair (R_z,L_q). This is a direct proof, not
opposite-infinite-+ transfer. QED.

These theorems strictly weaken the equality hypothesis of C087's
common-three-label corollary. They do not identify the two sets:
extra infinite labels on the larger side are expressly allowed.
They supply a class exclusion, not a new necessary-condition target.

## 3. An actual closed finite-LEFT block

Let F be a NONEMPTY PROPER vertex set, f=sum_{u in F}e_u, g=1-f.
Assume

\[
\operatorname{pd}_A L_u<\infty\ (u\in F),\qquad
gAf=0.
\tag{3.1}
\]

Set d=max_{u in F}pd_A L_u. The second condition says exactly that
each actual LEFT projective cover Ae_u, u in F, has composition
factors only in F. It is not inferred from finite pd of those
simples or from projectivity of their covers.

Put

\[
B=A/AfA\cong gAg,\qquad D=fAf.
\tag{3.2}
\]

The physical algebra has the literal triangular form

\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}B&0\\fAg&D\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{3.3}
\]

AfA=fA has only F LEFT composition factors, including the deleted
identities. It need not be radical, square-zero or projective.
C069/C074 are not applied to this kernel: the ordinary comparison
and infinity transfer are proved directly below. Simple right/left
B labels are the retained labels outside F.

### Theorem 3.1 — All-degree ordinary Tor comparison and exact retained labels

Under (3.1), for EVERY retained pair and EVERY n>=0 the natural
ordinary comparison is an isomorphism

\[
\operatorname{Tor}^A_n(R_z,L_y)
\xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{Tor}^B_n(R_z,L_y),
\qquad y,z\notin F.
\tag{3.4}
\]

The retained physical spaces are exactly unchanged:

\[
e_yBe_z=e_yAe_z\quad(y,z\notin F).
\tag{3.5}
\]

On retained labels,

\[
I_L(B)=I_L(A)\setminus F=I_L(A),\qquad
I_R(B)=I_R(A)\setminus F.
\tag{3.6}
\]

More precisely the retained RIGHT pds are identical, and if a
retained LEFT simple has finite b=pd_B L_y, then

\[
b\le\operatorname{pd}_A L_y\le b+d+1.
\tag{3.7}
\]

#### Proof: actual right resolutions and the natural ordinary map

For retained z, the actual right projective e_zA equals e_zB:
e_zAf=0 by gAf=0. Its entire right-module support stays outside F.
A kernel inside sums of these modules also has only retained right
factors, so its projective cover again uses only retained labels.
Induction constructs the SAME exact minimal right simple resolution
over A and B, with literally identical terms, differential matrices,
and augmentation. In particular its right pd is exactly the same.

Tensoring that actual A-projective resolution with B leaves it
unchanged and yields the B-projective resolution. The natural
change-of-rings map is represented by this literal base change,
so on every retained simple test it gives (3.4), not merely an
abstract equality of dimensions. No bounded Betti sampling is used.
Also gAfA g=0; hence the gAg block survives exactly and (3.5) holds.

#### Proof: the controlling finite side must be checked separately

K=AfA=fA has only F left simple factors, whose pds are at most d.
The valid finite-factor implication gives pd_A K<=d. Thus

\[
\operatorname{pd}_A B\le d+1.
\tag{3.8}
\]

If b=pd_B L_y is finite, inflating a length-b B-projective resolution
to A gives terms of pd at most d+1, and hence the upper bound in
(3.7). Conversely a finite A pd a makes all retained A-Tor tests
zero above a; (3.4) makes the ENTIRE minimal B term zero there,
so pd_B L_y<=a. This proves (3.7) and equivalence of finite/infinite
LEFT pd on retained labels. Since all deleted LEFT simples are
finite-pd by (3.1), I_L(A) loses none of its infinite labels.
The exact right resolution proof gives the right equality in (3.6).

Retained-pair comparison alone would not justify infinity preservation
of the LEFT source: deleted right detector tests are absent. The
finite ambient bound (3.8) supplies that missing direction explicitly.
QED.

The LEFT finite pd of the block also equals its finite D-simple
pd: modules supported in F have covers only Ae_u=De_u, so their
actual minimal left resolutions stay there. Thus gd D=d. This
fact is optional; it is not used as an unproved corner transfer.

### Corollary 3.2 — Genuine eligible count/dimension descent

Global infinity is equivalent for A and B. If gd B=b_0 is finite,
all retained A simple pds are at most b_0+d+1 and deleted pds at
most d, so

\[
\operatorname{gd}B\le\operatorname{gd}A\le b_0+d+1.
\tag{3.9}
\]

Any supported ordinary B tail is the SAME supported A tail by
(3.4)--(3.5). Therefore a bad A gives a bad B. This does not depend
on a derived or ordinary global-unit section.

The algebra dimension strictly decreases because F is nonempty.
The LEFT infinite count is unchanged, while the RIGHT infinite count
decreases EXACTLY by

\[
|F\cap I_R(A)|.
\tag{3.10}
\]

If this number is positive, it is a proved strict finite-count move,
and the sum of the two infinite counts also strictly decreases.
If it is zero, only the dimension/vertex count decreases; the theorem
does not call it a strict infinite-count move.

## 4. Direct closed finite-RIGHT block version

Assume instead pd_right,A R_u finite for u in F and fAg=0: each
actual RIGHT projective e_uA has only F right factors. Put
d_R=max_{u in F}pd_right,A R_u and B=gAg=A/AfA.

Now the retained actual LEFT projectives Ae_y equal Be_y and their
entire minimal left resolutions are unchanged. Thus the SAME natural
ordinary Tor isomorphism (3.4) holds in every retained pair/degree,
and the physical spaces (3.5) remain identical. The kernel AfA=Af
has only finite F RIGHT factors and pd_right,A K<=d_R, so
pd_right,A B<=d_R+1. Inflating a finite right B resolution and testing
all retained LEFT detectors proves exact retained RIGHT finite/infinite
labels. Retained LEFT pds are equal by the actual left resolution.

Hence global infinity and badness are preserved directly. The RIGHT
infinite count is unchanged, and the LEFT infinite count drops EXACTLY
by |F intersect I_L(A)|. Positive intersection gives a strict eligible
LEFT-count reduction; otherwise dimension still decreases. No use of
opposite-infinite-+ or equality of the two sets is made.

## 5. Inheritance of this actual block move

If one such eligible block F is deleted, every retained physical
Peirce space is identical by (3.5), and both infinite sets are the
restrictions of the original sets by (3.6) and its right analogue.
For another ORIGINAL eligible finite-side closed block G, the
remaining G minus F therefore still consists of finite-pd simples
on its controlling side, and the original physical zero condition
remains true on its retained matrix. If G minus F is nonempty proper,
the actual corresponding theorem applies again.

This proves eligibility inheritance for a CHECKED family of these
ordinary closed blocks, even mixing left- and right-finite blocks.
Every nonempty deletion lowers vertex count and dimension. A move
meeting an infinite label on its other side strictly lowers that
infinite count; no automatic block existence or generic coefficient
inheritance is asserted.

## 6. Arbitrary-size three-source closure from closed mismatch carriers

### Theorem 6.1

Let |I_L(A)|=3. Suppose every right-only infinite label

\[
z\in I_R(A)\setminus I_L(A)
\tag{6.1}
\]

belongs to some closed finite-LEFT block satisfying (3.1).
Then A is infinite +. Blocks need not include ALL finite-left
labels; their finite pds need not be zero; their dimensions and
number of vertices are unrestricted.

#### Proof

If I_L is contained in I_R, Theorem 2.1 already gives ordinary
visible return. Otherwise |I_L intersect I_R|<=2. If A were bad,
C080 gives |I_R|>=3, so the right-only mismatch set is nonempty.

Take the union F of the finitely many supplied left-finite closed
blocks. It is still left-finite, nonempty and proper because it
does not meet I_L. It is closed: each column belonging to one of
the blocks has zero entries outside that block, hence outside
their union. It contains every right-only infinite label.

The actual quotient B in Theorem 3.1 is therefore bad if A is bad,
but I_R(B)=I_R(A) minus F is contained in I_L(A) intersect I_R(A),
so has size at most two. Its RIGHT count strictly decreased by a
positive integer. C080 contradicts badness of B. QED.

In particular it suffices that the ENTIRE finite-left complement
F_L be projective-cover-closed (gAf=0). No projectivity of its
simples or socle condition is needed. A direct right theorem holds
for |I_R|=3 with left-only infinite labels carried by closed finite-RIGHT
blocks, using Theorem 2.2 and Section 4.

For arbitrary larger source counts, Sections 3--5 still provide a
genuine ordinary count move whenever such a block contains an
infinite label on its other side. They do not assert termination at
two from automatic availability of more blocks.

## 7. The actual obstruction examples admit a better block deletion

### T18's A42: delete the entire right-finite old block

Reuse C086's unchanged actual A42=[[B28,0],[V,Gamma]], with
I_L={4,5,v}, I_R={v}. ALL old RIGHT simples have finite pd<=2,
and their old rows have no v-column. Thus the old six-label block
F={0,1,2,3,4,5} satisfies Section 4's finite-RIGHT condition fAg=0.
Delete that WHOLE actual block. The quotient is Gamma of dimension
two; the retained left Av and its t_v minimal resolution are literally
unchanged. All-degree Tor(Rv,Lv) comparison is the identity.

The actual infinite counts are LEFT 3->1 and RIGHT 1->1. No new
infinite labels are created. This replaces the problematic partial
chain A42->C19->C10, where leaving formerly finite old labels behind
created ordinary periodic simples. The whole finite-side closed
block removes those labels along with the two infinite LEFT frames.
This repairs that example's reduction choice, not a false claim
that its partial maps inherit. A42 already has visible Tor; it is
not a bad counterexample. No new computation of its unchanged
certificates was needed.

### T19's loop/chain calibration: remove the left-finite carrier

The connected dimension-fourteen radical-square-zero example has
I_L={x1,x2,x3}, all six labels in I_R, and F={f1,f2,f3} of LEFT
pds 2,1,0. Its projective covers Ae_f have only F factors; no
x-row occurs in an F-column. Section 3 deletes this entire finite-pd
block and yields the product of three dual-number algebras,
dimension six. Counts are LEFT 3->3, RIGHT 6->3. The actual
ordinary comparison on retained pairs is an isomorphism in EVERY
degree. The quotient's two infinite sets are the common three
labels, so C087 or Theorem 2.1 closes it.

This handles the very calibration that fails T19's left socle
condition, with finite complement pd two rather than only zero.
Its actual right simple loops already give visible Tor, but the
new block theorem explains a valid strict right-count descent
without discarding finite-factor images in a stable category.

### Closed finite carriers are not automatic

In unchanged A42 the LEFT finite labels are 0,1,2,3. For every one
of them e_vAe_u is nonzero: the V row includes Hom from one of
the two projective R modules to that uniserial module. Since v is
LEFT infinite, no finite-LEFT projective-cover-closed block can
contain u. Thus finite pd of a simple alone does not put it in
an eligible same-side closed carrier. A42 has a valid RIGHT finite
closed carrier, a separate fact; no universal existence on either
side is concluded.

## 8. Normal adversarial self-audit

1. **Finite pd mistaken for physical closure?** (3.1) keeps BOTH
   hypotheses. A42 explicitly fails same-side closure for its
   left-finite simples. Finite projective-cover pd is not factor pd.
2. **Kernel incorrectly fed to C069/C074?** AfA contains f and
   need not be radical/square-zero. The comparison and ambient
   finite pd are proved directly; those radical theorems are not
   invoked for this kernel.
3. **Corner mistaken for quotient?** gAf=0 gives AfA=fA and
   gAfAg=0, so quotient and gAg are literally isomorphic. The
   retained Peirce spaces are IDENTICAL, not merely abstractly
   derived-related. The right case uses fAg=0 and AfA=Af.
4. **Natural map or just Betti dimensions?** A retained right
   projective is literally its B projective and the complete
   minimal resolution is unchanged. Tensor base change gives the
   canonical ordinary comparison, in every degree. The right
   version proves this through retained LEFT resolutions instead.

   Direct retained right resolutions give the right partition.
   The left direction from finite B to finite A needs pd_A B<=d+1;
   it is explicitly proved using the finite-factor kernel. This
   checks the deleted-detector gap rather than ignoring it.
6. **Count actually strict?** Only if the deleted finite-LEFT block
   meets I_R (or finite-RIGHT block meets I_L). Otherwise only
   dimension/vertex count is strict. The chosen finite-side count
   stays unchanged; exact removed opposite-side labels are stated.
7. **Badness preserved?** Global infinity follows from retained
   labelled pd equivalence and finiteness of every deleted chosen-side

8. **Comparison inheritance automatic?** Only this checked ordinary

9. **Containment silently strengthened to equality?** No. Theorem

10. **Three-source terminal step real?** If containment fails, the

11. **Coefficient perfectness or operation bounds assumed?** No.

12. **Arbitrary field and verification status?** All module actions,

## 9. Remaining closure issue

The actual finite-factor problem now has a valid ordinary reduction
in every projective-cover-closed finite-side carrier, with proved
count decrease when that carrier contains an opposite-side infinite
label. The whole three-source mismatch-carrier class closes, and
the larger-side containment class closes directly by ordinary return.

Outside those classes a finite-pd simple's projective cover can
contain infinite-pd factors and mix the carrier types; its finite
pd does not imply a closed block. A further global theorem must
produce a different eligible ordinary carrier/reduction or force
supported ordinary Tor from that interaction. This has not been
proved. Universal Gap A remains OPEN; the full goal stays active.
