# Run — Even ordinary Tor survives without a section

**Date:** 2026-10-03  
**Researcher:** one main Codex researcher  
**Evidence:** complete self-audited proofs; bounded exact rational calibration; not independently verified  
**Global status:** Universal Gap A remains OPEN. No novelty claim is made.

This continuation starts from T4. It seeks ordinary supported Tor rather
than an additional higher-output invariant. The new bridge does not assume
an algebra section or augmented formality. A parity obstruction in the
existing negative-weight bar filtration preserves every even-degree
quotient Tor class under an explicit bimodule hypothesis.

It excludes the projective-bimodule part of local-trivial screening and a
stated unbounded-cohomology class. The positive finite-bimodule-pd and
general nonperfect cases remain open.

## 1. Even visible recurrence in an ordinary algebra

Use the project's orientation: beta_{zy}(n)=dim Tor_n^C(R_z,L_y), and
reverse visibility means e_yCe_z!=0. Let

\[
b_n(C)=\sum_{y,z}\dim(e_yCe_z)\,\beta_{zy}(n),\qquad
t_n(C)=\sum_y\dim e_y\Omega_C^n L_y.
\]

Reuse the ordinary syzygy identity from the existing return machinery:

\[
b_n(C)=t_n(C)+t_{n+1}(C).
\tag{1.1}
\]

It follows by applying e_y to
0 -> Omega^{n+1} L_y -> P_n^y -> Omega^n L_y -> 0 and summing over y.
Every summand is a nonnegative integer.

### Lemma 1.1 — Some supported pair returns in infinitely many even degrees

If C is infinite +, then there is a fixed reverse-supported pair (z,y)
with Tor_{2r}^C(R_z,L_y)!=0 for infinitely many r.

#### Proof

If b_{2r} vanished for all sufficiently large r, (1.1) would make both
t_{2r} and t_{2r+1} vanish. Thus every high t_n and then every high b_n
would vanish, contrary to infinite +. Hence b_{2r} is nonzero infinitely
often. There are finitely many Peirce pairs, so one supported pair
contributes infinitely often. QED.

This does not say that every preselected persistent pair returns in even
degrees, or that diagonal simple Tor persists. The existing Saorin example
has only off-diagonal positive-degree simple Tor; no diagonal assertion
has been reintroduced.

## 2. The positive ordinary bridge

Let q be the retained connective derived quotient at x, let
C=H^0(q)=A/Ae_xA, and let S be the retained vertex algebra. Choose a
strictly augmented minimal S-relative A_infinity model

\[
H=C\oplus\bigoplus_{d\ge1}M_d[d],
\qquad M_d=H^{-d}(q).
\]

As before |m_r|=2-r, reduced C inputs lie in R=rad C, and a suspended
negative letter from M_d has homological degree d+1.

Assume

\[
\boxed{M_d=0\text{ for every positive even }d,\qquad
M_d\text{ is projective over }C^e\text{ for every odd }d.}
\tag{2.1}
\]

There is no bound on d, no finite-generation assumption, and no hypothesis
that the higher operations vanish. Since two odd negative degrees sum to
an even negative degree, (2.1) forces m_2 on two negative inputs to be zero.

### Theorem 2.1 — Every even quotient Tor class lifts

For every retained pair z,y and every r>=0, the natural comparison is
surjective:

\[
\boxed{
\epsilon_{2r}:\operatorname{Tor}^A_{2r}(R_z,L_y)
\longrightarrow\operatorname{Tor}^C_{2r}(R_z,L_y).
}
\tag{2.2}
\]

In particular

\[
\dim\operatorname{Tor}^A_{2r}(R_z,L_y)
\ge\dim\operatorname{Tor}^C_{2r}(R_z,L_y).
\tag{2.3}
\]

If C is infinite +, A is infinite +. This conclusion permits nonzero pure
higher operations and mixed higher operations.

#### Proof

The degree projection p:H -> C is a strict augmented A_infinity morphism,
as in T4. In the reduced bar complex B(H), let N be the kernel of B(p):
it consists of words containing at least one negative letter. It is a
subcomplex, since no higher contraction involving a negative input can
have degree-zero output.

Use the existing negative-weight filtration wt=sum d of the letters.
Contraction by m_r raises wt by r-2. The associated differential is b_2.
The filtration is finite in each total homological degree, since a word
has degree length+wt. This also holds on N.

For b_2, fix the sequence of negative letters
M_{d_1},...,M_{d_p}, p>=1. Negative-negative products are zero, so the
b_2 complex splits by this sequence. Its interspersed radical-C blocks
are the multibar complex computing

\[
R_z\otimes_C^{\mathbf L}M_{d_1}
\otimes_C^{\mathbf L}\cdots
\otimes_C^{\mathbf L}M_{d_p}
\otimes_C^{\mathbf L}L_y,
\tag{2.4}
\]

shifted by sum(d_i+1).

Each projective C-bimodule is a sum of summands Ce_i tensor_k e_jC.
Their tensor products over C remain projective C-bimodules, because

\[
(Ce_i\otimes_k e_jC)\otimes_C(Ce_a\otimes_k e_bC)
\cong Ce_i\otimes_k(e_jCe_a)\otimes_k e_bC.
\]

After tensoring the first such factor with R_z, the remaining object is
right C-projective. Continuing to tensor and finally applying L_y shows
that (2.4) has homology only in degree zero.
Every shift sum(d_i+1) is even. Thus the first page for N has zero in
every odd total homological degree.

Every later differential lowers total degree by one. The positive-weight
subcomplex's first page has only even total degrees, so all its later
differentials vanish and H_n(N)=0 for every odd n. Degreewise finite
filtration convergence makes this a statement about actual homology,
not only an associated-graded pattern.

The exact sequence

\[
0\longrightarrow N\longrightarrow B(H)\longrightarrow B(C)
\longrightarrow0
\]

now gives surjectivity H_{2r}(B(H)) -> H_{2r}(B(C)), because its
connecting target H_{2r-1}(N) is zero. The established derived quotient
comparison identifies Tor^H with ordinary Tor^A for the retained simples.
This proves (2.2).

Lemma 1.1 supplies a fixed supported even tail in C; (2.2) lifts it to A.
The reverse Peirce component also lifts through the ordinary quotient.
No singular or stable information is substituted for ordinary Tor. QED.

Surjectivity gives a vector-space splitting if one chooses one, but no
canonical chain section or algebra section is asserted.
The whole weight spectral sequence need not collapse: odd zero-weight
classes may still hit the even positive-weight homology.

## 3. Branch exclusions

### Corollary 3.1 — Local-trivial projective couplers cannot completely screen

In the two-stage local-trivial model

\[
H=C\oplus M[1],\qquad m_2,\quad m_3=\kappa,
\]

if M is projective as a C-bimodule, every even Tor_C class lifts to A,
regardless of kappa. Thus an infinite-+ ordinary quotient cannot be
completely screened.

This reuses the d=0 case of the existing Screening-Corridor theorem:
the positive columns have second coordinate zero, so a death from
degree n can only occur when n=2r+1. The new conclusion also uses
Lemma 1.1; one cannot screen the entire supported quotient tail by
destroying only odd degrees.

### Corollary 3.2 — Minimal-counterexample exclusion at arbitrary size

Let A be a globally vertex-minimal counterexample and choose x with
pd_A L_x infinite. Its retained derived quotient cannot satisfy (2.1).
In particular, the local-trivial branch cannot have a C^e-projective
first coupler.

#### Proof

UGA-C058 gives gldim C infinite using the actual tail of L_x, with no
opposite transfer. Minimality makes C infinite +. Theorem 2.1 would
make A infinite +, a contradiction. QED.

The general form permits unbounded odd negative cohomology, infinitely
generated cohomology, and nonzero higher-output indecomposables.
It is therefore an ordinary Tor survival mechanism beyond T4's
section/formality cases. The two-stage projective-bimodule case is a
whole excluded subbranch. Finite positive C^e-pd, arbitrary parity of
negative cohomology, and nonprojective couplers are not covered.

## 4. An actual non-formal calibration: separate projectivity is insufficient

The following algebra is an actual primitive deletion, not an arbitrary
filtered-complex or hypothetical A_infinity example.

Let Gamma=k[u]/u^4, U=Gamma/(u^2) as a left Gamma-module with generator b,
and V=Gamma/(u^2) as a right Gamma-module with generator a.
Form the Morita-context algebra

\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}\Gamma&U\\ V&k\end{pmatrix},
\]

with pairing U tensor_k V -> Gamma given by ba=u^2, and the opposite
pairing V tensor_Gamma U -> k zero. The image (u^2) annihilates U and V,
so the Morita compatibility identities and associativity hold over any
field.

Let y=0 be the Gamma vertex and x=1 the k vertex.
The basis is e_y,e_x,u,u2,u3,a,au,b,ub. The nonzero radical products are

\[
u^2=u2,\quad u\,u2=u2\,u=u3,\quad ub=u b,\quad au=a u,
\]

\[
ba=u2,\quad (ub)a=b(au)=u3.
\]

All other radical products vanish. This is the bound presentation
ba=u^2, ab=aub=0; these also force u^4=0.
The projectives are

\[
P_y=\langle e_y,u,u2,u3,a,au\rangle,\qquad
P_x=\langle e_x,b,ub\rangle.
\]

### 4.1 Actual minimal periodic resolutions

The following maps are right multiplication by the displayed elements.
For source y, repeat

\[
\begin{aligned}
d_1:P_y\oplus P_x\to P_y,\quad &(r,s)\mapsto ru+sa,\\
d_2:P_y\to P_y\oplus P_x,\quad &r\mapsto(ru,-rb),\\
d_3:P_x\to P_y,\quad &s\mapsto s(au),\\
d_4:P_y\to P_x,\quad &r\mapsto r(ub).
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.1}
\]

Their ranks are respectively 5,4,2,1. Adjacent compositions are zero,
and each image is the next kernel. The image of d_1 is JP_y, while
ker d_4=JP_y again. Every coefficient lies in J, so the resolution is
minimal and

\[
\Omega_A^4 L_y\cong L_y.
\tag{4.2}
\]

For example ker d_1 has basis
(u,-b),(u2,-ub),(u3,0),(au,0). The image of d_2 is exactly this space.
Its kernel is spanned by u3 and au, the image of d_3.
The kernel of d_3 is k(ub), which is L_y and is the image of d_4.
These explicit bases prove the ranks and exactness in every
characteristic, independently of the rational check below.

For source x, the first map is P_y -> P_x, r -> rb.
Its kernel is Z=span(a,au,u2,u3). The next two maps, repeated, are

\[
P_x^{\oplus2}\longrightarrow P_y,\quad(s,t)\mapsto sa+t(au),
\]

\[
P_y\longrightarrow P_x^{\oplus2},\quad r\mapsto(r(ub),-rb).
\tag{4.3}
\]

They have ranks 4 and 2 and are exact in alternation. The kernel of
the first is span((ub,-b),(0,ub)), isomorphic to Y=span(b,ub)=JP_x.
Thus Omega^3 L_x is isomorphic to Omega L_x and pd_A L_x is infinite.

For all n>=0 the source-y resolution has terms

\[
P_n^y=\begin{cases}
P_y,&n\equiv0,2\pmod4,\\
P_y\oplus P_x,&n\equiv1\pmod4,\\
P_x,&n\equiv3\pmod4.
\end{cases}
\]

For source x, P_0^x=P_x, P_n^x=P_y for positive odd n, and
P_n^x=P_x^{\oplus2} for positive even n.
Hence both simples have infinite pd and actual visible ordinary returns.
In particular Tor^A_{2r}(R_y,L_y)=k for every r>=0.

### 4.2 Coupler and non-formality

Delete x. Then e_xAe_x=k and C=k[u]/u^2. The multiplication

\[
U\otimes_k V\longrightarrow\Gamma
\]

has image span(u2,u3) and kernel M with basis

\[
v=ub\otimes a-b\otimes au,\qquad w=ub\otimes au.
\]

The C-bimodule action is

\[
uv=-w,\qquad vu=w,\qquad uw=wu=0.
\tag{4.4}
\]

M is free of rank one separately on each side, generated by v.
But C^e=k[l,r]/(l^2,r^2) is a four-dimensional local algebra; M has
dimension two, so is not projective over C^e. In fact it has infinite
C^e-pd, since a nonfree finite module over this local Artinian algebra
cannot have a terminating minimal free resolution.

The retained quotient has exactly C in degree zero and M in degree -1.
A homotopy for u^2 is b h a. The associativity obstruction on u,u,u is

\[
u(bha)-(bha)u=(ub)ha-bh(au),
\]

which represents v. Absorbing a common suspension sign in the basis,
the minimal model is

\[
H=C\oplus M[1],\qquad m_3(u,u,u)=v,\quad m_{r\ge4}=0.
\tag{4.5}
\]

All mixed m_3 vanish by degree. The normalized Hochschild equation is
uv+vu=0. A normalized 2-cochain with value alpha v+beta w has
coboundary on u,u,u lying in span(w), whereas (4.5) has value v.
Thus kappa is nonzero in every characteristic, and q is non-formal.

### 4.3 Every high quotient Tor class is killed, but new returns persist

For C, [u|...|u] spans Tor_n^C(k,k) in every degree.
Its first positive-weight differential in (4.5) is

\[
b_3[u|\cdots|u]
=\sum_{j=0}^{n-3}(-1)^j
[u^{|j}|v|u^{|n-3-j}]\quad(n\ge3),
\tag{4.6}
\]

where u^{|j} means j consecutive bar letters, not the product u^j.
The expression is a b_2 cycle. Every b_2 boundary in this one-M
column has its negative letter in span(w): a binary action can produce
w but not v. The distinct v-position words in (4.6) are therefore
nonzero in the first page.

All quotient Tor classes in degree n>=3 die on that first weight page.
Consequently the comparison Tor_n^A(R_y,L_y) -> Tor_n^C(k,k) has
zero image for every n>=3. Nevertheless (4.2) gives a new ordinary
diagonal return in every even degree, and in every degree congruent
to one modulo four. These returns lie in positive weight, not in the
original quotient edge image.

The example has a genuine infinite-pd deleted source and bounded
two-stage screening. It is infinite +, not a Gap-A counterexample and
not a realization of the unbounded-excursion Target-B branch.
It prevents replacing C^e-projectivity in Theorem 2.1 by projectivity,
even freeness, on the two separate sides.

## 6. Normal adversarial audit

1. The proof uses the existing negative-weight filtration, not the
   excursion filtration; their page conventions are not conflated.
2. Even supported recurrence is an aggregate consequence of (1.1);
   a fixed pair is selected only afterward. Diagonal simple Tor is
   neither assumed nor inferred in Lemma 1.1.
3. C^e-projectivity is essential to concentrate the multibar homology.
   Separate left/right projectivity is not silently substituted.
4. The parity argument also needs vanishing of every even negative
   cohomology degree. Without it negative-negative multiplication
   and odd total shifts need not vanish.
5. Unbounded negative degrees and unbounded higher arities are allowed.
   Degreewise finiteness suffices for the actual homology comparison.
6. Only the positive-weight subcomplex degenerates in this proof.
   Odd zero-weight classes may be screened on late pages.
7. The comparison proved is surjectivity in even degrees. No canonical
   section, formality or homological epimorphism A -> C is asserted.
8. Reverse Peirce visibility is inherited from the ordinary quotient,
   separately from Tor survival.
9. Minimal-counterexample use retains quotient infinity from the actual
   infinite-pd anchor and UGA-C058. No opposite transfer is used.
10. The nine-dimensional example is an actual finite algebra with
    completely checked module maps. Its model's non-formality and
    complete high quotient-edge screening do not make it a counterexample:
    the new ordinary returns are explicitly present.
11. The rational bounded computation does not certify all characteristics;
    the displayed basis proofs do.
12. No independent verifier acceptance or novelty is claimed.

## 7. Remaining closure work

There is now an ordinary supported-Tor bridge permitting pure non-formal
higher operations, not just a section criterion. It closes the
C^e-projective local-trivial coupler case and the explicit odd-negative,
projective-bimodule cohomology class, without bounds on negative degree
or operation arity.

The difficult remainder has nonprojective bimodule homology or both
parities. Finite positive C^e-pd gives the older screening corridor but
does not give the parity vanishing used here. In that case the next
high-value task is to turn the screened quotient tail into new ordinary
returns, as the calibration does through actual periodic kernels, or
to prove another strict descent. Do not merely assign another label to
the surviving obstruction.

The T4 section/descent results and T2 binary-indecomposable frontier
remain unchanged. Target A, local-trivial nonprojective screening and
the supported-weight branches remain open in their general forms.
