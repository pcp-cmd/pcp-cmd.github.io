## 2. The return-complement columns do not change

### Lemma 2.1 — Strict zero-column identities

There are compatible identities

\[
\boxed{\mathcal Q u=Au=Cu,\qquad
u\mathcal Q u=B=uCu,\qquad Ku=0.}
\]

#### Proof

Every degree-negative word in Q u ends with a factor in eAu, hence is
zero. Thus Q u=Au in degree zero. Also AeA u=Ae(eAu)=0, so the quotient

merely vanishing of cohomology. QED.

## 3. Exact comparison on real tails

### Theorem 3.1 — Tail comparison and defect annihilation

For every such X, regarded as a C-module and hence a Q-module,

\[
\mathcal Q\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}X\simeq X,\qquad
C\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}X\simeq X,\qquad
C\otimes_{\mathcal Q}^{\mathbf L}X\simeq X,
\]

\[
\boxed{K\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}X=0,\qquad
K\otimes_{\mathcal Q}^{\mathbf L}X=0.}
\]

For every left C-module Y, right C-module V, and n>=0,

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Ext}^n_A(X,Y)&\cong\operatorname{Ext}^n_C(X,Y)
\cong\operatorname{Ext}^n_{\mathcal Q}(X,Y),\\
\operatorname{Tor}^A_n(V,X)&\cong\operatorname{Tor}^C_n(V,X)
\cong\operatorname{Tor}^{\mathcal Q}_n(V,X).
\end{aligned}}
\]

Moreover pd_A X=pd_C X=pd_B T, including infinity.

#### Proof

Let P_B resolve T. By Tor orthogonality, Au tensor_B P_B is exact in
positive homological degrees with degree-zero homology X. Its terms are
minimal A-projectives. Lemma 2.1 identifies this same complex with
Cu tensor_B P_B and Q u tensor_B P_B. It is respectively a minimal
C-projective resolution and a homotopically projective dg Q-resolution
of X. Minimality follows from the radical coefficients in uJu; the
indecomposable columns and their radical maps are unchanged.

The three base-change statements are computed on these identical
resolutions. Tensor with K gives the zero complex, since Ku=0. For Hom
or tensor with Y or V, the degreewise adjunctions are the same: Hom from
Au, Cu or Q u is uY, and tensor to these columns is Vu. Thus their
computing complexes agree. The terms terminate in exactly the same
degree over A, C and B, proving the projective-dimension statement. QED.

This is a comparison on the specified real-tail class. It does not make
A -> C a homological epimorphism, or compare arbitrary C simples.
The full negative deletion defect is orthogonal to these tails; therefore
directly tensoring it with a real tail supplies no obstruction to the
higher-output residue K_H from the previous Run. The symbols K and K_H
