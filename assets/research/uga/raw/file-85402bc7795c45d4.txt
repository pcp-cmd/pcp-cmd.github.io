# REGRESSION GATES

These are not separate test runs. Check them during normal adversarial audit of any new universal lemma.

## RG-001 — Graph/support recurrence is not homological recurrence
Repeated vertices, graph cycles, SCC recurrence, or repeated labels do not by themselves imply nonzero Tor/Ext/Yoneda return. Require an actual chain-level, derived, spectral-sequence, or comparison mechanism.

## RG-002 — Singular/Tate/stable information is not ordinary Tor/Ext
Do not promote singular-category or Tate recurrence to an ordinary simple-simple persistent Tor/Ext witness without an explicit comparison theorem.

## RG-003 — Corner/local nonvanishing does not automatically survive ambiently
Local or corner Tor can be screened in the ambient algebra. Any corner-to-ambient step must exhibit the comparison/survival mechanism.

## RG-004 — Homological survival and Peirce visibility are distinct
Even an ambient persistent Tor group is not automatically an infinite-\(+\) witness; check the required reverse Peirce support.

## RG-005 — Opposite-side \(+\) transfer is quarantined
The two-sided finite-head package contains a project-derived opposite-invariance argument, while older project guards distinguish \(+\) from co\(+\). Until independently reconciled, do not silently treat this as settled external fact.

## RG-006 — Finite-dimensionality does not automatically bound higher \(A_\infty\) arity
Do not close the Massey branch by assuming a finite-dimensional algebra or derived quotient has only finitely many nonzero higher operations.

## RG-007 — Linear combinations matter
Membership in \(\sum_r \operatorname{im}m_r\) does not justify selecting one higher-product output. Use the repaired subspace/Nakayama argument where applicable.

## RG-008 — Every primitive edge is required by the necklace criterion
UGA-C049 requires every edge of an already established cycle to have nonzero primitive image. One primitive edge alone is insufficient; the correction Run gives an explicit minimal boundary necklace with a primitive negative edge.

## RG-009 — Eventual primitive vanishing is not all-degree vanishing
High-degree primitive vanishing does not remove low-degree negative primitive seeds. Tree degrees include the sum of primitive leaf magnitudes as well as Massey excess. The five-dimensional formal Auslander calibration has unbounded negative homology generated by a single degree-minus-one leaf with zero excess.

## RG-010 — Formality and the two filtrations must be stated precisely
UGA-C055 assumes augmented formality over the retained vertex algebra and applies to the excursion/bar-length filtration. It does not follow from finite-dimensionality or a finite operation-arity list, and it does not assert negative-weight collapse.

## RG-011 — Finite generation and zero-column scope
