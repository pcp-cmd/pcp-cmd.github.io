## 1. Setup and reusable comparison

Let A be an elementary finite-dimensional algebra over k. Delete a primitive
idempotent e=e_x, retain f=1-e, and let S be the product of the retained
vertex fields. Use the normalized retained derived quotient Q from the
previous Run, Section 8.1. It is connective and augmented over S, and each
graded piece is finite-dimensional. Its augmentation ideal is degree-zero
fJf together with the negative passages

\[
Q^{-n}=fAe\otimes_k(eJe)^{\otimes_k(n-1)}\otimes_k eAf
\qquad(n\ge1).
\]

Expansion of passages gives the actual ambient bar complex on retained
endpoints, with

\[
\text{reduced-bar length}=\text{x-excursion}+1
\]

in positive homological degrees. This is the chain and filtered
identification already proved in UGA-C055; it is not an identification
with the separate negative-weight filtration.

Choose a minimal strictly unital augmented A-infinity model
H=H^*(Q). Write C=H^0, R=rad C, and

\[
\bar H=R\oplus\bigoplus_{d\ge1}H^{-d},\qquad
D_2=\operatorname{span}\{m_2(a,b):a,b\in\bar H\},\qquad
I_2(H)=\bar H/D_2.
\]

The product m_2 is the ordinary graded cohomology product. Thus I_2 is the
binary indecomposable quotient of the augmented graded associative algebra;
it is independent of choices of higher operations under an augmented
cohomology-algebra identification. This invariance assertion does not
extend to the all-operation quotient P_infinity.

Bar homological degree of a word with negative magnitudes d_1,...,d_l is
l+sum d_i. In particular [eta], eta in H^{-d}, has degree d+1. Denote
excursion columns by p and total homological degree by n.

## 2. Exact second-page description

### Theorem 2.1 — Zero-column binary quotient

For every retained pair (z,y) and every n>=1,

\[
\boxed{E^2_{0,n}(z,y)\cong e_z I_2(H)^{-(n-1)}e_y.}
\]

A zero-excursion class is screened at the first differential precisely when
its cohomology class lies in D_2. Such a class has a filler of excursion at
most one in the actual ambient bar complex.

#### Proof

An augmented A-infinity quasi-isomorphism H -> Q induces a bar map which
does not increase length: each Taylor component groups a consecutive
block of inputs into one output. On the associated graded its linear
component induces the tensor powers of a quasi-isomorphism. Since tensor
products are over the semisimple algebra S, it gives the same length
spectral sequence from E^1 onward. Connectiveness bounds length in every
fixed total degree, so no completion is needed.

In the minimal model m_1=0. The E^1 differential is b_2, which lowers
length by one. On length one it has no outgoing component. Its incoming
image from length two is exactly the span of m_2 products on reduced
inputs, in the specified Peirce component and degree. This proves the
formula, including n=1 where it is R/R^2.

If eta=sum_i m_2(a_i,b_i), the sum of the corresponding signed two-letter
words has full bar differential [eta]: no b_r with r>=3 can act on two
letters. Its image under the bar map has length at most two. Any change of
one-letter representative by an internal Q boundary can be corrected with
a one-letter chain. Through the passage comparison this gives an actual
ambient filler of excursion at most one. Conversely such a filler gives
the incoming d_1 image, hence membership in D_2. QED.

For the high-degree local identification UGA-C045, n=m+2 and d=m+1.
Thus no shift of one degree is suppressed in applying this theorem to
Tor_m^{eAe}(e_zAe,eAe_y).

## 3. Finite generation excludes unbounded screening

### Lemma 3.1 — Binary generators and degree bounds

For a degreewise finite connective augmented graded algebra H with
finite-dimensional C and nilpotent R, the following are equivalent:

1. H is generated by finitely many homogeneous elements as an associative
   algebra over S.
2. I_2(H)^{-d}=0 for all d>d_0, for some finite d_0.

#### Proof

If H has finitely many homogeneous generators, replace their degree-zero
parts by elements of R after subtracting S components. Modulo D_2 every
word of reduced length at least two vanishes. Therefore the images of
those finitely many generators span I_2, proving the bounded-degree
statement.

Conversely, choose finite homogeneous lifts V_d of I_2^{-d} for
0<=d<=d_0; this is possible by degreewise finiteness. Lifts V_0 of R/R^2
generate C over S by radical nilpotence. Suppose all negative magnitudes
below d have already been generated, and put M_d=H^{-d}. The degree-d
part of the quotient definition gives

\[
M_d=V_d+RM_d+M_dR+
\sum_{i=1}^{d-1}m_2(M_i,M_{d-i}),
\]

with V_d=0 for d>d_0. Let L_d be the C-bimodule spanned by V_d and these
lower-degree binary products. Associativity makes the lower-product span
stable under both C actions. For N_d=M_d/L_d the displayed equation says
N_d=RN_d+N_dR. If R^t=0, the ideal

\[
\mathfrak r=R\otimes C^{op}+C\otimes R^{op}\subset C^e
\]

satisfies mathfrak r^{2t-1}=0. Iteration forces N_d=0. Hence M_d=L_d,
which lies in the algebra already generated by the chosen lifts and C.
Induction proves generation by the finite union of the V_d. QED.

### Corollary 3.2 — Conditional exclusion for Target B

If H^*(Q_x) is finitely generated as an augmented graded associative
S-algebra, every zero-excursion class with n-1>d_0 is screened at d_1.
For local Tor this is m+1>d_0. Consequently unbounded excursion screening
requires an infinitely generated cohomology algebra.

This requires no formality assumption. It is a statement about the high
zero-excursion column, not collapse of the entire spectral sequence at
E^2. Higher differentials between other columns may still occur.

Combining with UGA-C055, Target B requires both augmented non-formality
and infinitely generated graded cohomology. Neither property alone is a
sufficient screening mechanism.

## 4. The surviving indecomposables must be higher outputs

For the fixed minimal model set

\[
D_\infty=\sum_{r\ge2}\operatorname{im}m_r,qquad
P_\infty=\bar H/D_\infty,qquad
K_H=D_\infty/D_2.
\]

These are graded S-bimodule quotients of spans of outputs. No ideal or
cohomology-algebra structure on D_infinity or K_H is asserted. There is
an exact sequence

\[
\boxed{0\longrightarrow K_H\longrightarrow I_2(H)
\longrightarrow P_\infty\longrightarrow0.}
\]

### Proposition 4.1 — Necessary higher-output residue

If a nonzero E^2 zero-column class represented by eta is eventually killed
in the full bar complex, then its binary-quotient image is a nonzero
element of K_H. Therefore an unbounded-screening subsequence forces
K_H^{-d}!=0 in unbounded degrees on the fixed local pair.

#### Proof

Every one-letter word is a cycle in the minimal model. If [eta] is a full
bar boundary, its length-one component is a sum of outputs m_r. Thus
eta belongs to D_infinity. Its nonzero E^2 image says eta does not belong
to D_2, proving the assertion. This argument takes the entire span of
outputs; it does not select an individual operation from a linear sum.
For a spectral-sequence death, the same full-boundary conclusion holds:
the one-letter cycle lies in the lowest positive-degree filtration piece,
and finite convergence identifies E^infinity there with its actual
filtered homology image. There is no lower piece to hide a surviving class.
An unbounded-screening subsequence has first hits beyond d_1 in unbounded
degrees, so applies to these nonzero binary-quotient classes. QED.

In a hypothetical non-infinite-+ algebra, UGA-C054 also gives a uniform
N with

\[
e_z(P_\infty)^{-d}e_y=0
\quad(d+1\ge N,\ e_yAe_z\ne0).
\]

Hence in every high reverse-supported pair the exact sequence identifies

\[
e_zI_2(H)^{-d}e_y=e_zK_H^{-d}e_y
\]

as subquotients under the displayed inclusion. No reverse support of the
particular mixed-linker pair is assumed: the unbounded-screening
necessity above follows from its actual death, even for an invisible pair.

The residue K_H is only a necessary condition. A higher-operation output
can have other bar-differential components which obstruct a full filler.
Its presence neither proves actual late screening nor supplies supported
ordinary Tor. K_H may depend on the chosen higher model; I_2 does not.

## 5. Finite-dimensional A does not imply finite generation of H

Consider the radical-square-zero algebra with vertices x,y, a loop t at x,
an arrow a:y->x, and an arrow b:x->y. Its basis is

\[
e_x,e_y,t,a,b,qquad J^2=0.
\]

This is an associative five-dimensional ordinary algebra. Delete x. Its
corner is Gamma=k[t]/(t^2); the retained degree-zero corner is k e_y. In
the normalized model from Section 1, for every n>=1,

\[
Q^{-n}=k u_n,qquad
u_n=b h(t h)^{n-1}a.
\]

Every normalized differential is zero: it multiplies a pair of radical
factors, including ba in degree -1. Every product u_p u_q is also zero:
concatenation uses the radical product ab=0. Thus this actual quotient is
formal with

\[
H=k e_y\oplus\bigoplus_{n\ge1}k u_n,qquad
|u_n|=-n,qquad \bar H^2=0.
\]

It has infinitely many binary generators. The ambient simple bar
differential is zero as well, so retained diagonal passages survive in
every degree at least two, and the loop t gives deleted-vertex diagonal
Tor in every positive degree. In particular pd_A L_x=infinity and A is
infinite +. This is not a Gap-A counterexample and has no screening; it
shows that finite-dimensionality and the infinite-pd anchor alone do not
prove finite generation of derived-quotient cohomology.

The prior five-dimensional calibration is different: its nonzero product
ab=t produces formal k<u> and bounded binary generators with unbounded
negative homology. Together the two exact examples distinguish bounded
binary generators, bounded negative cohomology, formality, and ambient
survival. No new numerical calculation is needed for this J^2=0 example.

## 6. Normal adversarial self-audit

1. **Filtration:** the input is the proved passage/length identification.
   The conclusion is about excursion, not negative weight.
2. **Page and degree:** E^1 length-one classes are H^{-(n-1)}; incoming
   b_2 gives precisely D_2. For local Tor, d=m+1 and n=m+2.
3. **Representatives:** binary products have actual two-letter fillers.
   The A-infinity comparison and internal-boundary correction preserve
   the required length bound.
4. **Algebra generation:** degreewise finiteness and finite C are explicit.
   Two-sided radical nilpotence removes same-degree transport in the
   induction; an unjustified positive-degree induction is not used.
5. **Scope:** finite generation eliminates high zero-column E^2 classes.
   It does not eliminate every higher differential of a non-formal model.
6. **Model invariance:** the binary quotient is a graded-ring construction.
   Higher-output K_H and P_infinity retain the fixed-model qualification.
7. **Linear combinations:** every quotient uses a whole linear span.
   Actual death implies membership in D_infinity, not one chosen output.
8. **Necessity only:** K_H membership is not a full-boundary certificate.
   Actual excursion correction chains remain part of the open question.
9. **Visibility:** neither support cycles nor invisible tails are promoted
   to an infinite-+ witness. The displayed visible identification uses the
   reverse support e_yAe_z!=0 and the derived Tor comparison.
10. **Calibration:** the square-zero example is infinite + with surviving
    tails; the old Auslander example has finite global dimension. Neither
    realizes a non-+ infinitely-pd screening counterexample.
11. **Arity:** an unbounded screening page is not identified with the arity
    of a single operation. Chains of corrections must be retained. The
    existing deletion-normal-form filtered-complex ladder already warns
    against arity/page shortcuts in its own negative-weight filtration;
    its page indices are not transported here.
12. **Dependence:** the new lemmas use C055's passage comparison and C054
    only for the optional visible-pair consequence. They do not use the
    quarantined opposite-invariance step. An application of the original
    local finite-head existence theorem still retains its source gate.
13. **Evidence:** proofs have one author; no independent audit, native MRS
    verifier acceptance, or universal computation is claimed.

## 7. Compressed frontier and next action

Target B must retain all of the following:

- the actual infinite-pd deleted-vertex anchor;
- an augmented non-formal derived quotient with infinitely generated
  graded associative cohomology;
- an unbounded binary-indecomposable tail on the actual mixed-linker pair,
  lying in K_H rather than surviving as P_infinity primitives;
- arbitrarily late genuine excursion fillers;
- vanishing of every high reverse-supported ordinary Tor group in the
  hypothetical non-+ ambient algebra.

The new exclusion is a strict compression of the unresolved mechanism,
not closure of Universal Gap A. Finite-dimensionality alone cannot close
it, as Section 5 demonstrates. The next load-bearing question is whether
the actual quotient's boundary-module Tor orthogonality and infinite-pd
anchor prohibit such an unbounded higher-output indecomposable tail, or
force a supported ordinary survivor. The corner-orthogonality theorem
does not yet supply this bridge.

The bounded-screening Target A, local-trivial deletion, and supported
negative-weight branches remain open; the opposite-source gate is
unchanged. No claim of literature novelty is made.
