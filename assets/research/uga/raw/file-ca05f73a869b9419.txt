## 1. Conventions and reused ordinary interface

Let A be finite-dimensional elementary over an arbitrary field k.
Write L_y for left simples, R_z for right simples, and

\[
\beta_{zy}(n)=\dim_k\operatorname{Tor}^A_n(R_z,L_y),\qquad
D(y,z)=\dim_k e_yAe_z.
\]

A visible ordinary tail is one fixed pair (z,y) with D(y,z)>0
and beta_zy(n)>0 in infinitely many n. A bad algebra has infinite
global dimension and no such tail. I_L(A) and I_R(A) are the separate
sets of infinite-pd left and right simple labels.

For a vertex block S put C_S=A/Ae_SA. E_N(A,C_S) means that the natural
ordinary same-pair Tor map is onto for EVERY retained simple pair
and EVERY EVEN n>=N. This is the exact C083 interface. Reuse:

- C080: |I_L|<=2 or |I_R|<=2 closes Gap A at every finite size.
- C083: E_N prevents new infinite labels on BOTH sides, lifts an
  ordinary supported infinite tail, and makes deletion of a current
  infinite-pd simple a badness-preserving strict count move for a bad A.
- C084: nested ordinary quotient maps compose. Surjectivity of the
  composite implies surjectivity of the SECOND map; it says nothing
  about surjectivity of the first map.

No opposite-infinite-+ transfer, graph-return inference, stable/Tate
comparison, section or coefficient inheritance is used below.

## 2. Only the original infinite-source blocks needed before count two

### Theorem 2.1 — Infinite-source block comparison closes an all-size class

Choose the LEFT side and set I=I_L(A), p=|I|. If p<=2, use C080.
If p>=3, suppose for every nonempty block

\[
S\subseteq I,\qquad |S|\le p-2,
\tag{2.1}
\]

there is a finite N_S with E_{N_S}(A,C_S). Then A satisfies Gap A.
The same theorem holds with the independently defined RIGHT set
I_R(A) and direct right deletions.

There is no comparison hypothesis on blocks containing an originally
finite-pd chosen-side label, or on larger blocks. There is no assertion
that arbitrary quotients inherit a class of coefficient hypotheses.
All retained Tor TEST pairs in each required comparison are still
needed; (2.1) narrows deleted blocks, not detector pairs.

#### Proof

Assume A is bad. During descent let T be the set of labels already
deleted and use the actual quotient C_T. Initially T is empty.
Every successful C083 move preserves badness, and its no-new-label
inclusion ensures

\[
I_L(C_T)\subseteq I\setminus T.
\tag{2.2}
\]

If C_T has at most two infinite left labels, C080 already contradicts
badness. Otherwise choose x in I_L(C_T). After t=|T| moves, the current
count is at most p-t. A current count at least three therefore implies
t<=p-3. The block S=T union {x} lies in I and has size at most p-2.

The required original comparison A->C_S is onto at every even degree
n>=N_S. Its ordinary bar map factors through A->C_T->C_S. Consequently
the SECOND map C_T->C_S is onto there, on all its retained tests.
C083 applies to the actual infinite source x of the current bad C_T.
It keeps C_S bad and strictly decreases the chosen infinite count;
it also cannot create new right infinite labels. This establishes the
next (2.2). At most p-2 successful moves reach count at most two,
contradicting C080. All blocks used are proper because |S|<=p-2<=v-2.

For a right-chosen chain repeat this argument using actual right
minimal resolutions, I_R(A), and C083's direct right statement. No
equality of left and right infinite sets is needed. QED.

### Corollary 2.2 — One eligible deletion closes a three-source branch

If |I_L(A)|=3 and ANY ONE actual infinite left source x has
E_N(A,A/Ae_xA), then A satisfies Gap A: if it were bad, C083 would
produce a bad quotient with at most two infinite left labels.
There is a direct right version. This requires neither an inherited
comparison nor comparisons at the other two infinite sources.

Theorem 2.1 is a strictly weaker hypothesis than C084's comparisons
for every original block. Section 5 gives an actual A with p=3 which
has every initial primitive comparison, but fails one two-source
block comparison arbitrarily late. It therefore distinguishes the
two hypotheses. No new quantitative global-dimension bound is
asserted under Theorem 2.1; C084's all-block quantitative result keeps
its original hypotheses.

## 3. A 28-dimensional finite-global-dimension calibration

Let R be the oriented two-vertex cycle algebra with all paths of
length three zero. Its uniserial modules M_{s,l}, s=0,1 and l=1,2,3,
have a basis at positions 0,...,l-1; position j has vertex s+j mod 2,
and successive arrows shift the basis one position. Order them as

\[
(0,1),(1,1),(0,2),(1,2),(0,3),(1,3)
\quad\leftrightarrow\quad 0,1,2,3,4,5.
\]

Put B=End_R(direct sum M_{s,l})^op. The Hom basis h_{ij}^a sends the
generator of M_i to position a of M_j, where

\[
\max(0,l_j-l_i)\le a<l_j,\qquad s_j+a=s_i\pmod2.
\tag{3.1}
\]

In B, h_{ij}^a belongs to e_iBe_j and

\[
h_{ij}^a h_{jk}^b=h_{ik}^{a+b}\quad(a+b<l_k),
\tag{3.2}
\]

and other products are zero. This literal table has dimension 28
over every field. Nonidentity Hom basis elements span its nilpotent
radical; the six h_{ii}^0 are primitive identities.

For l=1,2 the exact sequence

\[
0\longrightarrow M_{s+1,l}
\longrightarrow M_{s,l+1}\oplus M_{s+1,l-1}
\longrightarrow M_{s,l}\longrightarrow0
\tag{3.3}
\]

uses (shift-one injection, quotient) and (quotient, minus shift-one
injection), with M_{*,0}=0. Checking generator positions proves
exactness in every characteristic. Every nonisomorphism from an M_i
to M_{s,l} factors through the last map: for l=1 its nonidentity
position-zero maps lift to M_{s,2}; for l=2 a position-zero map comes
from M_{s,3}, while a position-one map factors through M_{s+1,1}.
Thus applying Hom_R(direct sum M,-) gives an actual minimal left
B-projective simple resolution of length two. For l=3 all radical
maps land in rad M_{s,3}=M_{s+1,2}, giving length one. Hence the left
simple pds are (2,2,2,2,1,1). Since all left simple tests have pd<=2,
the whole degree-three term of every minimal right simple resolution
is zero. Thus gd B=2 without an opposite-infinite-+ argument.

The physical primitive quotients B_h=B/Be_hB have the following
complete left simple pds. The retained label order is displayed.

| Deleted h | dim B_h | retained labels | left simple pds |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 0 | 19 | 1,2,3,4,5 | 1,2,2,1,1 |
| 1 | 19 | 0,2,3,4,5 | 1,2,2,1,1 |
| 2 | 12 | 0,1,3,4,5 | 0,2,1,1,0 |
| 3 | 12 | 0,1,2,4,5 | 2,0,1,0,1 |
| 4 | 14 | 0,1,2,3,5 | 4,2,3,2,1 |
| 5 | 14 | 0,1,2,3,4 | 2,4,2,3,1 |

These numbers are certified by complete actual integral projective
cover sequences, terminating at the stated degree; see Section 6.
They are not pd guesses from a finite Betti prefix. The same all-left
test argument bounds every right pd by four. Consequently all initial
primitive ordinary Tor comparisons for B are isomorphisms in EVERY
degree n>=5 (both sides are zero).

However D=B/B(e4+e5)B has dimension eight and radical square zero.
The certificates identify its actual left simple syzygies as

\[
\Omega L_0=L_2,\quad\Omega L_2=L_1,\quad
\Omega L_1=L_3,\quad\Omega L_3=L_0.
\tag{3.4}
\]

These are module isomorphisms with minimal covers, so
Tor^D_{4r}(R0,L0)=k for every r>=1. This conclusion uses actual
syzygies, not an inference from the four-cycle support graph.
The composite block comparison B->D therefore fails at every cutoff.
Initial primitive comparisons do not automatically inherit even
when the starting algebra has finite global dimension.

## 4. Add actual infinite sources and test a remaining infinite deletion

Let Gamma=k[t_v]/(t_v^2), G=Gamma/(t_v), and

\[
V=G\otimes_k(e_4B\oplus e_5B),\qquad
A=\begin{pmatrix}B&0\\V&\Gamma\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{4.1}
\]

V is an actual bimodule: t_vV=0 and its right action is the direct
sum of two regular projective B rows. It has dimension twelve, so
dim A=42. The new vertex is v (index 6). This algebra is connected.

The complete actual left cover certificates prove

\[
\Omega_A L_4=Ae_3\oplus L_v,\qquad
\Omega_A L_5=Ae_2\oplus L_v,\qquad
\Omega_A L_v=L_v.
\tag{4.2}
\]

For the first two identities, multiplication by h_{3,4}^1 and
h_{2,5}^1 embeds the displayed projective into the radical of the
simple's cover. The remaining V-generator is the identity of its
own frame, a copy of L_v. The direct sums and embeddings are checked
as A-linear maps with inverse certificates. The other four left
simples have pd two, either by the exact induction of (3.3) using
right-projective V or by their complete integral covers. Therefore

\[
I_L(A)=\{4,5,v\}.
\tag{4.3}
\]

For the actual right covers,

\[
\Omega_{A^{op}}R_v=e_4A\oplus e_5A\oplus R_v.
\tag{4.4}
\]

All old right projectives and resolutions are precisely their B
rows, so their pds are 2,2,2,2,1,1. Hence I_R(A)={v}. Iterating (4.4)
gives one copy of R_v in every positive syzygy and a projective
e_vA term in every degree. In particular

\[
\operatorname{Tor}^A_n(R_v,L_v)=k\quad\text{for EVERY }n\ge0.
\tag{4.5}
\]

A is already infinite +. It is not a bad algebra or a Gap-A
counterexample. The left/right sets in (4.3)--(4.4) are distinct.

### All initial primitive comparisons are eventual FULL isomorphisms

For each old deletion h, physical ideal closure gives

\[

\begin{pmatrix}

\end{pmatrix},
\tag{4.6}
\]

where a removed frame contributes zero. The quotient normal form is

All other retained rows are zero on both sides in every n>=5. If h=v,

## 5. Why the new positive condition is strictly weaker

A in (4.1) has p=3 and every initial primitive comparison. Thus it
satisfies Theorem 2.1: only singleton blocks of its original I_L
are required since p-2=1. But it does NOT satisfy C084's all-block
comparison hypothesis, because the block {4,5} in (4.9) fails
arbitrarily late. These are strictly different classes of comparison
hypotheses. The example's Gap-A conclusion was already supplied
directly by (4.5) and C079's right-singleton closure; no novelty of
that individual conclusion is claimed.

Theorem 2.1 does not need to take the second deletion in (4.10): the
first eligible move already reaches C080. An inheritance counterexample
after that terminal point therefore does not refute the theorem.
For p>3, larger original infinite-source blocks are retained exactly
because initial primitive comparisons do not automatically supply
the needed subsequent comparisons.

### Integral-cover lemma

For each cover, the result includes integer module action matrices,
integer radical and top-lift bases, actual cover images, integer
kernel inclusions, projective actions and kernel actions. It verifies:

1. Radical basis plus top lifts is an integer basis with integer
   inverse, in every vertex component.
2. Chosen image columns form an integer basis of the entire
   codomain, with integer inverse.
3. Kernel columns plus the corresponding chosen domain unit vectors
   form an integer basis of the entire domain, with integer inverse.
4. The cover is A-linear, surjective and minimal; kernel actions
   are the induced actions; full module multiplication is checked.

An integer square matrix with integer inverse is unimodular. All
these coordinate changes remain invertible over EVERY field.
The split exact sequence as abelian groups in items 2--3 therefore
base-changes to an exact vector-space sequence in every characteristic,
with the displayed A-action and identical kernel module. Item 1
preserves the radical quotient and hence minimality. Repeating these
finitely many certified steps proves the stated finite projective
dimensions over every field. The terminal cover has zero kernel;
all earlier minimal projective terms are nonzero, giving exact pds.

The actual infinite-frame isomorphisms also have integer matrices
and integer inverse, so (3.4), (4.2), (4.4) and (4.8) survive every
characteristic. Their infinite consequences follow from written

## 7. Normal adversarial self-audit

- **Wrong map in a composite?** Only the second map is inferred onto.
  Every required original S block is assumed; no inference gives it
  from original singleton maps.
- **New current source outside the original I?** C083 prevents this
  on the chosen side after every actual successful move; (2.2) is
  an induction invariant. The theorem does not assume the converse.
- **Lost detector pairs?** E_N still uses ALL retained pairs. Neither
  source-block narrowing nor a supported-only test replaces them.
- **Count might stop at zero?** Badness includes infinite global
  dimension and is preserved by C083. In any event C080 excludes
  every current count at most two. The chain has at most p-2 moves.
- **Deleting finite sources in the negative example?** Both 4 in A
  and 5 in C are infinite LEFT sources by literal (4.2), (4.8).
  They are not claimed infinite on the right.
- **Corner mistaken for quotient?** Full actual ideal closure is
  checked. B_4 and D have dimensions fourteen and eight, distinct
  from merely taking a corner.
- **Cycle mistaken for ordinary homology?** (3.4) consists of actual
  simple kernel isomorphisms and minimal covers. It implies the
  ordinary diagonal Tor tail by iteration, independently of graph
  recurrence. Direct right simple cycles give the right counts.
- **Initial comparison inferred from pd labels alone?** The R_v row
  is infinite and is controlled by actual right resolution base
  change. Only the old finite rows use their terminating covers.
- **Field-dependent rank?** Every cover basis change and inverse is
  integral unimodular; the all-field lemma above is required.
- **Section assumed?** The low-degree Tor obstruction proves none
  for the first move; high comparison starts at degree five.
- **Global dichotomy falsely declared broken or proved?** A already
  has (4.5). This counterexample breaks automatic inheritance, not
  the bad-algebra global alternative. Theorem 2.1 closes its exact
  comparison class, not every finite-dimensional algebra.
- **Terminal case mistaken for a needed next move?** The negative
  example's first move reaches two sources. Theorem 2.1 stops there;
  it does not require the failing second move.
- **Earlier frontier erased?** C076's ordinary supported radical

## 8. What now needs an actual closure bridge

The general target remains: for a bad A with at least three infinite
simple labels on BOTH sides, force an ordinary visible return or an
eligible reduction. When an even comparison is available at a
three-source vertex, Corollary 2.2 closes that branch immediately.
For larger counts, Theorem 2.1 shows precisely which original
infinite-source blocks can certify the finite descent.

The counterexample rules out declaring all such comparisons inherited
from initial primitives. A genuinely global advance must now exploit
the badness hypothesis to produce the needed ordinary comparison,
construct a different badness-preserving smaller counterexample, or
force supported ordinary Tor directly. No additional derived invariant
is introduced at this checkpoint. Universal Gap A remains OPEN.
