# Initial master guidance — literature-blind cycle

Transport is explicitly `off`; this is Main-Codex-authored operational guidance,
not an independent strategy-model reply and not mathematical evidence.

## Shared objective

Determine, without assuming either truth value, whether eventual vanishing of
Hochschild homology for a finite-dimensional complex algebra forces finite
global dimension. A positive resolution requires a complete general proof. A
negative resolution requires an ordinary finite-dimensional complex algebra for
which both eventual vanishing and infinite global dimension are independently
verified.

## Hard Phase-I boundary

- Do not browse, search, cite, or open literature.
- Do not ask Main Codex for hints about known results.
- Do not rely on a remembered named claim unless its full hypotheses and proof
  are reconstructed; label remembered background explicitly.
- Publish substantive attempts, obstructions, candidate examples, and dead ends
  to shared memory.
- Submit only complete, self-contained mathematical claims to the independent
  verifier.

## Distinct branches

1. **Positive mechanism.** Formalize the exact statement and try to derive
   perfection/finite projective dimensions from eventual Hochschild-homology
   vanishing. Identify the first genuinely missing implication rather than
   hiding it behind a slogan.
2. **Negative construction.** Search independently for an ordinary
   finite-dimensional complex algebra separating eventual Hochschild-homology
   vanishing from finite global dimension. Track all candidate properties as
   separate proof obligations.
3. **Adversarial structural analysis.** Stress-test both directions, seek
   invariant-theoretic blind spots, and audit any shared proposal for field,
   dimension, grading, vanishing-range, and global-dimension errors.

## Cycle stopping condition

Complete one substantive literature-blind round per worker, persist findings and
dead ends, and then stop for Freeze 001. No worker proceeds to literature until
Main Codex has written and re-read the independent-progress snapshot.
