## 2. Actual projective ideal powers

### Lemma 2.1 — Tensor multiplication is an actual isomorphism

For r>=2, tensor the actual RIGHT exact sequence

\[
0\longrightarrow I^{r-1}\longrightarrow A\longrightarrow A/I^{r-1}\longrightarrow0
\]

with the LEFT-flat SECOND factor I. The resulting injection into A tensor_A I=I is actual multiplication, whose image is I^r. Thus

\[
I^{r-1}\otimes_A I\xrightarrow[\text{multiplication}]{\cong}I^r.
\tag{2.3}
\]

These are bimodule isomorphisms. No vanishing is assigned to a tensor product merely because the physical product is zero.

Using (2.2) for the second factor, induction gives

\[
I^re_j\cong\bigoplus_i(Ae_i)^{(T^r)_{ij}}.
\tag{2.4}
\]

Indeed the next tensor term is a direct sum of I^(r-1)e_i with multiplicities T_ij. In particular EVERY I^r is LEFT-projective.

Since I is radical, its physical powers eventually vanish. Equation (2.4) therefore makes the nonnegative integer matrix T nilpotent. A nilpotent N by N matrix satisfies T^N=0, so

\[

\tag{2.5}

## 3. Automatic strictly smaller closed support

Balance and RIGHT nilpotent Nakayama give

Thus F is precisely the set of nonzero ROWS of T. It is nonempty because I is nonzero.

\[

\tag{3.2}
