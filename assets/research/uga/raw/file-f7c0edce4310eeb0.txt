# RESEARCH GOVERNANCE — preserved project lesson

Parallel chat windows and incomplete handoffs previously caused route rediscovery and status drift. The final archive therefore treats research-state management as a first-class asset.

Required future discipline:

1. one canonical current state;
2. append-only historical record;
3. explicit provenance for every imported result;
4. a DO-NOT-REINVENT index;
5. explicit handoff snapshots;
6. retrieve prior assets before opening a new route;
7. preserve failed/refuted routes instead of deleting them;
8. keep mathematical state separate from research-policy state;
9. do not promote a claim because a later route “needs” it;
10. retain UTF-8/Unicode path integrity in every repackage.
