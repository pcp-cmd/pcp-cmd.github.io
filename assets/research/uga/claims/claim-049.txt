## UGA-C049 — Primitive-Necklace Survival
**Status:** PROJECT-DERIVED / SELF-AUDITED / NOT INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED  
**Source:** final Derived-Necklace Run, Theorem 13.1

If every edge of a minimal necklace survives nontrivially in
\[
P_\infty=\bar H/\sum_{r\ge2}\operatorname{im}m_r,
\]
then the necklace cycle is not a bar boundary.
