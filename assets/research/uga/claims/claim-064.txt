## UGA-C064 — Coefficient Vanishing Forces a Section and a Finite Obstruction Cutoff
**Status:** COMPLETE SELF-AUDITED ALL-DEGREE PROOF / NOT INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED  
**Source:** Run-Hochschild-Obstruction-Cutoff-and-Ordinary-Tor-2026-10-03.md, Lemma 2.1, Theorems 2.2 / 3.2 / 4.2 and Corollaries 2.3 / 4.3--4.4.

For the retained augmented minimal model H with H^0=C and M_d=H^{-d}, vanishing HH^{d+2}(C,M_d)=0 for every d>=1 constructs an actual augmented, vertex-preserving A_infinity section C -> H. At arity n the first unresolved morphism defect is a cocycle with coefficient M_{n-2}; the correcting component is s_{n-1}. It includes all mixed-operation terms generated by earlier corrections, rather than only the raw pure-input m_n. Direct-sum conilpotence makes the infinite recursion an actual bar map. C060 then gives a split ordinary Tor retraction in every degree and reverse-supported pair.

If id_{C^e} C^e<=D<infinity and each M_d has finite C^e-pd, all section obstructions at arities n>D vanish. A section exists exactly when its equations admit a compatible solution through arity D; for D<=2 that condition is automatic. This is a uniform bound on section obstruction arity, not an operation, screening-page or excursion bound.

In particular, if both regular injective dimensions of C are at most one and every M_d has finite C^e-pd, the full ordinary Tor bridge holds. Both negative parities, negative-negative products, mixed operations, unbounded degrees and individual positive finite pd are allowed. A global vertex-minimal counterexample cannot have such a deletion at an infinite-pd vertex. The perfect two-stage local-trivial coupler subbranch over a one-Gorenstein quotient is excluded, extending C062 beyond projective couplers.

No general regular injective bound, coefficient perfectness, derived-section inheritance or independent verification is asserted. For a many-layer model only a section, not full formality, is proved. No opposite-side infinite-+ transfer is used.
