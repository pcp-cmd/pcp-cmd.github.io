## UGA-C050 — Nakayama Elimination of Same-Degree Transport
**Status:** PROJECT-DERIVED / SELF-AUDITED / NOT INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED  
**Source:** final Derived-Necklace Run, Lemmas 15.1–15.2

For \(C=H^0(Q_x)\), \(R=\operatorname{rad}C\), \(M_d=H^{-d}(Q_x)\), if the primitive quotient vanishes in degree \(-d\), then
\[
M_d=G_d,
\]
where \(G_d\) is generated from higher operations whose negative inputs have strictly smaller negative magnitude. Same-degree radical transport is inessential.

**Depends on:** UGA-C049 plus the degreewise decomposition in Section 15.
