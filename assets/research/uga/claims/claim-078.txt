Its actual left projective-column matrix is T=D-I with D(i,j)=dim e_iAe_j, and M^{tensor_A p} has actual columns T^p, concentrated in derived degree zero.

In the unchanged A5 algebra, name the dual-number vertex u and the other w. Then D=((2,1),(1,1)), T=((1,1),(1,0)), and M has actual tensor dimensions 8,13,21,..., nonzero for every p>=1. Nevertheless gldim A5=2 and every ordinary simple-pair Tor vanishes for n>=3. Repeated tensoring need not preserve minimality.
